# MandiGuard: Empirical Measurement of Phantom Spatial Arbitrage and a Risk-Gated Dispatch Decision-Support System for Indian Mandis

[![Python 3.11](https://img.shields.io/badge/Python-3.11-blue.svg)](environment/requirements.txt)
[![Tests](https://img.shields.io/badge/pytest-23%20passed-brightgreen.svg)](results/pytest_execution.log)

**`MandiGuard`** investigates and mitigates **Phantom Spatial Arbitrage** across 6 major Indian agricultural states (Maharashtra, Uttar Pradesh, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Gujarat, and Himachal Pradesh) and 4 perishable commodities (Onion, Potato, Tomato, and Apple), evaluated over 2024–2026 and stress-tested across nearly 10 years (2017–2026: 42,722,187 raw AGMARKNET archive records condensed to 883,406 balanced panel rows across 178 core mandis, verified in Dual-Tier `Exp 14` against official `data.gov.in` Resource `9ef84268-d588-465a-a308-a864a43d0070` on 2023-10-18 [100.00% `Min`/`Max`, 99.42% `Modal` across 172 keys] and 149-day AGMARKNET 2.0 direct official exports [100.00% `Min`/`Max`/`Modal` across 2,042 matched keys], plus 22 live Nov-2026 queries).

Empirically (`Exp 1`, `Exp 10`), most apparent cross-mandi portal spreads erode once silent or closed destinations, variety mismatches, real highway freight, and ambient weather spoilage are counted. Rather than chasing naive price-board spreads or unfiltered ML point forecasts, `MandiGuard` operates as a **risk-gated dispatch decision-support system** (`T1 -> T2 -> T3 -> T4` deterministic four-step verification cascade + a frozen `HistGradientBoostingRegressor` price-return model + a causal empirical conformal safety cushion, with no LLM or RL policy). It anchors decisions to public AGMARKNET archives, OSRM highway routes, ECMWF ERA5 and NASA POWER climate grids, and Government of India survey parameters—emitting either `DISPATCH` or `REFUSE_PHANTOM_SPREAD` (`SELL_LOCAL`).

## Key Results Around 4 Minimal Metrics (Mean Net Gain, Loss-Trip Rate, Worst-10% Loss, Live API Calls/Query)

### View 1: Main Holdout Benchmark (`N = 69,526`, Modeled Trap Penalties)
* **Spread Erosion (`Exp 1`):** Across 63,040 naive screen-chasing dispatches (`B1`), headline spreads average ₹1,330.49/qtl. **46.69%** have no exact-variety quote for three arrival days. The flag `closed_or_no_variety_trap` is **58.94%** because it also counts exact-variety sales after a 1–2 day wait (12.25 pp). A missing row is not an observed closure. **40.46%** have negative gain under the structural cost model (episode-level `CVaR_10%` -₹1,062.66/qtl, including non-dispatches in the benchmark table's other policies).
* **`H1` (`SUPPORTED`, $p_{\text{Holm}} = 0.0012$):** **`+₹102.78/qtl`** higher net profit than naive screen chasing (`B1`: ₹452.79 vs ₹350.01/qtl) on 69,526 shortlisted holdout episodes, equal to **+₹3,083.5 extra profit per 30-quintal smallholder load** (81.2% of average monthly household net crop income of ₹3,798 per MoSPI NSS 77th Round).
* **`H2` (`NOT_SUPPORTED`, $p_{\text{Holm}} = 0.2699$):** `+₹6.13/qtl` over fresh same-day OSRM heuristics (`B2`: ₹452.79 vs ₹446.66/qtl, 95% CI [-₹17.02, +₹25.02])—statistically indistinguishable from noise on mean profit because `MandiGuard` refuses risky trips (48.66% vs 62.11% dispatch), while nearly halving loss-trip rate (9.97% vs 18.11%) and reducing worst-10% `CVaR_10%` from -₹436.67/qtl to -₹37.89/qtl. *(An exploratory check on the 42,766 trips in the 4 states with 100% archived NWP forecasts yields +₹31.82/qtl, confirming northern Ridge weather estimation did not drive results; it does not rescue `H2`.)*
* **`H3` (`SUPPORTED`, $p_{\text{Holm}} = 0.0008$):** Cuts **episode-level** loss incidence by **37.29 pp** (`B3` 42.14% → `MandiGuard` 4.85%). The conditional loss-trip rates, 45.28% and 9.97%, are a different denominator (dispatched trips only) and differ by 35.31 pp.
* **`H4` (`SUPPORTED`, $p_{\text{Holm}} = 0.0008$):** Matches upfront all-tool profit (₹452.79/qtl) using **44.4% fewer calls** (`8.75` vs `15.74` calls/episode)—an expected design consequence of ordering cheap checks first that is practically useful under tight query budgets (B = 6: +₹104.54/qtl over `B4`), though direct API rupee savings (modeled at ₹0.05/call) are minor relative to crop profit.

### View 2: Fair Matched Complete-Observation Cohorts & Trap-Penalty Crossover (`Exp 15`)
* **Fair Matched Guaranteed-Open Cohort (`6_Fair_Matched_AllTopK_Observed_d0`, N = 10,162 identical episodes):** Evaluating an identical episode set where every top-5 candidate has observed arrival-day `d0` prices (zero simulated trap rules, zero post-outcome selection bias), `MandiGuard` (₹510.89/qtl, 6.82% LTR, -₹36.07 `CVaR_10%`) beats `B1` (₹472.51/qtl, +₹38.38) and `B2` (₹469.99/qtl, +₹40.91). Unfiltered point-ML `B3` (₹553.22/qtl, 14.77% LTR, -₹362.40 `CVaR_10%`) earns +₹42.33/qtl higher mean profit when all yards are guaranteed open—at 2.2x higher loss-trip rate and 10x deeper tail losses. On 3-day matched episodes (`7_Fair_Matched_AllTopK_Observed_3d`, N = 16,849), `MandiGuard` earns ₹592.53/qtl (7.74% LTR) vs `B1` ₹581.06 (+₹11.47), `B2` ₹572.09 (+₹20.44), and `B3` ₹670.38 (15.02% LTR). *Key insight:* price forecasting itself works well on open yards; standard point-ML fails on the full market specifically because it lacks `T1` openness screening and `T4` downside cushions.
* **Trap-Penalty Crossover Curve (N = 69,526, Regimes 1–5):** `MandiGuard` leads `B1` and `B3` from 1.5x return freight / 10% discount (+₹102.78 / +₹121.94), through 1.0x / 10% (+₹91.66 / +₹109.39) and 0.5x / 5% (+₹42.43 / +₹46.12), as well as under a ₹0 break-even phone-ahead assumption on missing-3d trips (+₹82.26 / +₹73.61). Mean profit crosses below `B1`/`B3` only at the unrealistic zero-cost boundary (0.0x return freight and 0% variety discount: -₹10.00 vs `B1`, -₹20.93 vs `B3`), where `MandiGuard` still cuts loss-trip rate roughly three-fold to 6.09% (vs 18.52% `B1` and 20.40% `B3`).

### Decade Robustness, Official Dual-Tier Provenance, Stage-II `MandiGuard-CRC` & Appendix Illustrations
* **10-Year Multi-Regime Stability & Scaling (`Exp 10`, `Exp 10b`):** Across 661,192 matched episodes (2017H2–2026), `MandiGuard` holds Loss-Trip Rate to 8.78%–12.03% vs 39.18%–44.29% naive (+₹165.14/qtl gain over `B1` during the 2024 El-Niño heatwave), while 1-year recent calibration (₹452.79/qtl) captures 99.5% of 8-year training utility (₹454.94/qtl).
* **Dual-Tier Official Data Parity (`Exp 14`) & Live Queries (`Exp 8`):** Tier-1 `data.gov.in` XML spot-check (`2023-10-18`, 172 matched keys) confirms 100.00% `Min_Price`/`Max_Price` and 99.42% `Modal_Price` parity; Tier-2 direct AGMARKNET 2.0 portal export (`hp_apple_daily_2025_2026.csv`, **149 dates** `2025-11-07..2026-04-13`) confirms **100.00% (2,042/2,042) exact parity** across `Min`/`Max`/`Modal`, while 22 live Nov-2026 queries show 11/22 corridors non-reporting for 7 days.
* **Stage II — Mechanistic Autopsy (`Exp 16`) & Validation-Calibrated `MandiGuard-CRC` (`Exp 17`, `[POST-DIAGNOSTIC / VALIDATION-CALIBRATED]`):** Proves that complete-case `dropna` drops 55.83% of `Train_2024` corridors (understating Q20 downside 1.82x–2.00x in Train / up to 2.54x in Holdout), pooled LCB causes 89%–90% calm `Q1` over-coverage vs 68% volatile `Q4` under-coverage, and rigid `T1` rolling thresholds (not `T4` width) blocked 77.82%–82.75% of HP Apple `MG_v1` refusals vs `B2`. Replacing censored LCB with uncensored execution + price-scaled CRC ($\lambda^* = 0.15$ frozen on `Val_2025H1`) + thin-grade-aware `T1` raises holdout net gain to **₹479.17/qtl** (`+₹129.16` vs `B1`, `+₹32.51` vs `B2`, `+₹26.38` vs `MG_v1`, 10.48% LTR, `-₹44.03` CVaR), raises open-yard `N=10,162` gain to **₹533.75/qtl**, and lifts HP Apple gain `+34.2%` to **₹812.08/qtl**.
* **Appendix Illustrations (`Exp 11`–`Exp 13b`, Exploratory — Not Main Contributions):** The manuscript Appendix separately illustrates plug-in quantile/adaptive conformal models (`MandiGuard_ACI`, reducing HP Apple worst-10% loss from -₹102.76 to -₹74.42/qtl), local `ZeroCloud` road/CPU caching (3.84–5.53 live calls), cooperative 90-qtl FPO pooling (₹477.28/qtl under 0.70x modeled freight), and $Q_{10}$ spoilage countdown SMS cards.
