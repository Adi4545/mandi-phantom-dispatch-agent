# Submission readiness

**Supervisor review is appropriate for the retrospective comparison.** The revised manuscript and [correction record](CORRECTIONS.md) supersede the earlier readiness assessment. Code execution can verify modeled gains, chosen route keys and expected shortfall. It cannot establish the prices a farmer would have obtained, the operational value of forecasts, or Stage II generalization to a new season.

Before submission, reconcile institution-specific thesis formatting, secure supervisor sign-off, check redistribution terms and remove superseded figures or teaching PDFs from the submission bundle. Before claims about deployment, collect timestamped quotes and forecast vintages. Before claims about farmer benefit, obtain trip-level receipts and a credible comparison design.
