# Questions for the corrected MandiGuard defense

**What is the strongest finding?** On the 69,526 shortlisted episodes and under the reference settlement model, Stage I earned an additional modeled ₹102.78 per quintal over a stale-price rule. Against the stronger fresh-price and road-cost comparator, the difference was ₹6.13 with a 95% interval of −₹17.02 to +₹25.02.

**Why can a mean gain coexist with deep losses?** The mean includes every shortlisted episode, including local sales scored at zero. The lower-decile mean looks at exactly the worst tenth of that distribution. Among 20 episodes with one loss of ₹100, 17 zeros and two gains, the two worst outcomes average −₹50.

**Why did the correction matter?** With frequent abstention, the 10th percentile can be zero. Averaging every payoff at or below zero puts too much weight on abstentions. In the six-state v1 evaluation that produced −₹37.89. The exact lower-decile mean is −₹212.93.

**What is the route-selection error?** Suppose A beats B on score. A has no arrival-day quote and B does. A column-by-column choice can return A's name and B's payoff. Retaining A's entire row preserves the missing value. On the graded apple panel, B1's corrected arrival-day mean uses just 90 of 1,537 dispatches.

**Can we infer a market closure from a missing price?** The observed fact is a missing report for the relevant market, date and variety. A market can trade another variety or fail to publish a quote. Diversion, waiting and discounts are settlement assumptions.

**How important was the weather input?** Fixed decision weather changed 1,058 v1 actions or destinations on the six-state panel. The modeled mean rose ₹1.82 per quintal while the losing-trip share rose from 9.97% to 10.42%. Historical issue times remain unknown.

**What does Stage II establish?** A validation-calibrated policy scored higher on average on previously inspected evaluation data. Its exact lower tail worsened from −₹212.93 to −₹223.19 in the main cohort. The 10.48% losing-trip rate exceeded its 9.5% validation target. New temporal or geographic data would be needed for independent validation.

**Why use a deterministic cascade?** Each gate has an inspectable input and refusal condition. The modeled call saving against upfront checks follows from skipping later steps on rejected routes. A deployed system would need actual latency and billing measurements.

**What is original, and what would prove farmer benefit?** The contribution is an empirical comparison of policies under exact-variety reporting gaps and stated payoff assumptions. Spatial trade frictions, forecasting and abstention have established precedents. A prospective comparison should record time-stamped options, actual dispatch, buyer receipts, lot quality, freight and the local alternative, with a design that can distinguish policy effects from farmer selection.
