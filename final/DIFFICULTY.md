# Implementation Complexity & Conceptual Simplicity Analysis (Revision v7)

This project demonstrates that an operational market dispatch safeguard does **not** require deep neural architectures or opaque parameter search when arrival missing-quote geometry dominates prediction variance (consistent with $1/N$ heuristic robustness in DeMiguel et al. 2009 and Gigerenzer & Gaissmaier 2011).

## 1. Algorithmic Simplicity Profile (`4.3/5` -> `1.6/5` -> `1.2/5`)
- **Zero-Parameter Operational Rules (`m = 0`):** Once candidate corridor distances (`F_road = 35.0 + 0.45 * h_s * road_km`) and strictly prior reporting frequencies (`rep28`, `p_dow`) and volatility (`cv28`, `0.21%` sparse fallback on `77` episodes when `cnt28 < 2`) are read, `Safe_EV_Sunk_m0`, `Safe_EV_Phys_m0`, and `RT_2F_Min_m0` evaluate each menu in sub-millisecond arithmetic. Note that argmax corridor selection still operates in additive INR units (`p_dow * G_1SD - F_road`); switching to ratio argmax changes `5,113` (`14.03%`) dispatches (`v7_exp4`).
- **Why Learned & OR Alternatives Were Evaluated Honestly (`v7_exp4`):** Pre-registered Stage I hypothesis `H2` (`PREREG_A10.md`) tested a learned `HistGradientBoostingRegressor` ranking pipeline (`4.3/5`), which proved inconclusive (`+₹6.13/qtl`, Holm `p = 0.2699`). Even after removing weather leaks and purging overlapping validation horizons (`−939` eps, `v7_exp4`), zero-parameter `Safe_EV_Sunk_m0` (`cond_ES10 −₹447.48`, EpLoss `14.88%`) outperforms retrained `Ridge` (`−₹530.30`), `HGBR_EV_TV_age13` (`−₹523.53`), `HGBR_RiskAverse_L500` (`−₹512.43`), `OR_Vargas_MeanVar_g0002` (`−₹496.76`), and intact `MG_T1_T2_T3_T4_Intact_matched` (`−₹531.66`) on conditional tail risk while remaining far simpler (`m = 0`).
- **Matched vs Tuned Baselines:** Compared against validation-tuned baselines (`MG_matched` at `th = ₹133.03`, `ES10 −₹508.72`; `RR_Abstain_matched` at `κ* = 5.0`, `m* = ₹5.40`, `ES10 −₹459.46`), `Safe_EV_Sunk_m0` reaches `ES10 −₹345.53/qtl` (`+₹179.24` over `B2`; `+₹91.67` when dispatch-matched under `PREREG_A18` `H_A18_2`, `p_holm_2s_prereg4 = 0.0040`).

## 2. Structural Sensitivity & Operational Limits
- **Sensitivity Grid (`36` cells) & Coupled Phase 6 (`v7_exp1..5`):** Across `18` freight, HP hill, *arhtiya* commission (`0–6%`), decay (`0.5–2x`), detention (`0–100`), `joint_optimistic`, `joint_harsh`, and `outside_origin_lag` perturbations (`36` cells on `171` markets), `Safe_EV_Sunk_m0` maintains strictly positive gains in all `36/36` cells (`Mean` `+₹15.03` to `+₹61.22/qtl`; `ES10` `+₹144.52` to `+₹323.60/qtl`). Under Phase 6 `<= t-2` latency (`N = 57,047`, `t2_age_2_3`), `Safe_EV_Sunk_m0` preserves `ES10 −₹364.48` vs `−₹508.36` (`p = 0.001`) and `cond_ES10 −₹484.32` vs `−₹672.39` (`+₹188.07`), whereas `RT_2F_Min_m0` loses `cond_ES10` significance (`p = 0.9805`). Under coupled `S6_Frozen x Stoch_CondMAR_p1.00`, `Safe_EV` retains `+₹366.85 cond_ES10` (`−₹595.27` vs `−₹962.12`).
- **Clear Failure Boundaries:** Practitioners should **not** expect Mean elevation under Open-MAR (`>= 25.45%` ambient / `26.65%` ERA5), `3`-day hindsight (`B2 ₹549.81` vs `Safe_EV ₹533.14`), `div = 0.0` free return (`p_2s = 0.2199`), SUTVA `10%` fleet crowding (`Mean −₹5.31`), or `HP B=12` (`cond_ES10 p = 0.1239`) / Apple (`N = 2,802`, `41.38%` loss, `cond_ES10 p = 0.0940` NS).
