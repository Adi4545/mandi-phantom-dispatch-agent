1. **Modeled Payoffs $\ne$ Farmer Cash Income (`R7`):** Zero physical freight receipts, weighment bills, or mandi auction *patti* slips exist in AGMARKNET. Road freight (`₹35 + 0.45 * hp * km`), loading (`₹35`), detention (`₹25/qtl-day`), and `Q10` spoilage are survey-anchored approximations (`NITI Aayog 2021`, `NABCONS 2022`, `GoHP Economic Survey 2025–26`, `RBI WP 08/2024`).

Therefore our rules act strictly as an upstream 6 a.m. alert filter before live phone confirmation.

Both `PREREG_A10.md` (which also retained an unedited line-32 note preserved untouched for audit history) and `PREREG_A18.md` are **locked exploratory follow-up specifications** on an already-exposed 2025–2026 calendar, pending prospective FPO field testing.
