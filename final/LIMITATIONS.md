# Scope Constraints and Methodological Limitations (Revision v8)

This document bounds every empirical claim in `paper/manuscript.md` under fourteen documented limitations so no reader confuses simulator counterfactuals or tail-risk gains with universal Mean claims or live transaction receipts:

1. **Observational Proxy for Physical Closures & Coupled Manski Bounds:** AGMARKNET records reporting gaps rather than gate-camera labels. In `171` primary test markets (`N = 57,634`), naive `B1_lagged_screen` resolves sequentially to `40,217` (`69.78%`) exact day-`0` sales, `284` (`0.49%`: `227 d1 + 57 d2`) waits, `609` (`1.06%`) day-`2` cross-variety fallbacks at `0.90 * settle_any_min_d2`, and `16,524` (`28.67%`: `15,960 d0 + 364 d1 + 200 d2`) emergency diversions. Under coupled Manski bounds (`v7_exp3`), worst-case Mean shifts turn deeply negative (`−₹124.95`, `−₹199.51`, `−₹325.40/qtl` across `Supp1..3`), bracketed against the exact `25.45%` Open-MAR break-even (`26.65%` ERA5), while coupled Manski `cond_ES10` remains positive in `Supp1` (`[+₹152.20, +₹328.28]`) and `Supp2` (`[+₹105.96, +₹394.89]` vs decoupled `−₹0.51`).
2. **Retrospective Discovery, `2018–2023` Reverse-Survivorship & Blocked Gate J:** Zero-tuned-parameter rules (`Safe_EV_Sunk_m0`, `Safe_EV_Phys_m0`, `RT_2F_Min_m0`; `9` fixed structural choices incl. `z = 1.0`, sunk freight `-1.0F`) were formulated after Stage I `2024` inspection, leaving `2025–2026` as **specification-locked exploratory inference** and prospective live validation (`Gate J`) explicitly `BLOCKED`. Revision v8 adds clean non-lookahead retrospective evaluation across `166` markets (`2018–2023`, `N = 383,952`) and `115` `2017`-active markets (`N = 325,993`), explicitly noting reverse-survivorship drops `481/647` historical yards absent from the `2024` frame alongside the `7` post-`2024` lookahead yards in the legacy `173`-market (`N = 389,357`) extract.
3. **Zero-Diversion & Hindsight Regimes Exclude Mean Gains:** When emergency return penalties on `d0 = 0` are zeroed out (`div = 0.0` Mean `+₹12.03/qtl`, `p_2s = 0.2199` NS; zero-floor return `+₹11.21/qtl`, `p_2s = 0.2559` NS), or under `3`-day hindsight settlement (`B2` Mean `₹549.81` vs `Safe_EV ₹533.14`), Mean gains vanish or reverse even though `ES10` tail protection remains strongly positive (`+₹169.82` to `+₹308.41/qtl`). Mean also turns negative in `PreChoice_AllCandidates_ExactD0` (`N = 24,654`, `−₹20.74/qtl`, `+₹260.20` `ES10`).
4. **Tail-Risk (`ES10` / `cond_ES10`) Nuances, UP `q10=0` Atom & Finite-Sample `CGM G/(G-1)` (`v7_splits_171`, `v7_exp5`):** While `Safe_EV_Sunk_m0` improves unconditional `ES10` across `6/6` states and `5/5` crop slices (`NonApple`, Onion, Potato, Tomato, Apple), UP exhibits an unconditional `q10 = 0` atom (`7.28% < 10%` loss rate, making `cond_ES10` essential), and finite-sample `CGM G/(G-1)` + `p_wild_webb6_1w_block_only` confirms **HP (`B = 12`, `g_star_block = 1.535` warning) fails significance on `cond_ES10`** (`+₹250.82`, `p = 0.1239`, `p_stud = 0.18791`, `p_cgm_gstar = 0.29947`) and **Apple (`B = 23`) is borderline on `cond_ES10`** (`+₹462.22`, `p = 0.0550`, `p_stud = 0.05947`, `p_cgm_gstar = 0.07443`). Subgroup Means cross zero in GJ (`p = 0.940`), MH (`p = 0.160`), UP (`p = 0.116`), Tomato (`p = 0.877`), and Apple (`p = 0.143`), and Potato `Safe_EV_Sunk_m0` (`ES10 −₹242.00`) trails `RR_Abstain_matched` (`−₹224.80`). Across `2018–2023`, `Safe_EV` passes `ES10` & `cond_ES10` in `6/6` years (`p = 0.001`), while **`2020` (`+₹12.93`, `p = 0.2079`) and `2023` (`+₹20.35`, `p = 0.0770`) Means are non-significant** (and `RT_2F_Min_m0` `cond_ES10` fails pooled `b2dest` at `p = 0.079` and `2020/2021/2023` at `p = 0.265–0.528`).
5. **Dual-Metric & Selection Critique (`cond_ES10`, `D_S ∩ D_B`):** Unconditional `ES10` averages zero-valued abstentions into the `10%` cutoff; therefore Revision v8 pairs every `ES10` headline with **`cond_ES10` among dispatched trips** (`Safe_EV_Sunk_m0 −₹447.48` vs `B2 −₹628.37` = `+₹180.89`, nearly identical to `Safe_EV_Phys_m0 −₹447.33`). Explicitly, **`cond_ES10` is NOT selection-free**: we decompose it directly on `CommonIntersection_DS_cap_DB` (`n = 33,398`: `Safe_EV ₹773.38` vs `B2 ₹707.72`, `+₹65.66` Mean, `+₹53.40` `ES10` `−₹464.82` vs `−₹518.22`; pruned `7,944` `B2`-only trips average `₹68.96`, `58.60%` loss, `ES10 −₹1,018.70`) alongside `PreChoice_AllCandidates_ExactD0`.
6. **Two-Day Portal Publication Latency (`v7_exp1`):** True AGMARKNET portal upload timestamps are unavailable historically. Stressing `<= t - 2` (`t2_age_2_3`, `N = 57,047`, `th_b2 = 300.0`) confirms `Safe_EV_Sunk_m0` retains `ES10` (`−₹364.48` vs `−₹508.36`, `p = 0.001`) and `cond_ES10` (`−₹484.32` vs `−₹672.39`, `+₹188.07`, `p = 0.001`), whereas `RT_2F_Min_m0` loses `ES10` (`p = 0.3628`) and `cond_ES10` (`p = 0.9805`).
7. **Stylized Cost, Spoilage & Outsider Price-Discrimination Parameters (`v7_exp2`, `v7_exp5`):** Road freight (`₹35.0 + 0.45 * h_s * road_km`, `30 qtl` load assumption, benchmarked vs NITI Aayog/RMI `₹0.258/qtl-km`), stylized UP/state seller-fee tiers (`0.5%–1.5%`, anchored qualitatively to `PIB relid=154554`), and operational 24h decay benchmarks (`0.86%–3.25%`, calibrated from NABCONS `PIB PRID=2151371` cumulative `"Total loss in market level"` shares) are stylized simulator parameters rather than empirical receipts. Under fixed `_spoil_return_leg` coupled worst-case smallholder assumptions (`S6_Smallholder_4pctArh_USDA_ERA5_Frozen`), closed settlement yields `Safe_EV` `₹307.39 / −₹576.57 / −₹725.73` vs `B2 ₹247.15 / −₹832.00 / −₹969.71`, while `S6_Frozen x Stoch_CondMAR_p1.00` retains `+₹366.85 cond_ES10` (`−₹595.27` vs `−₹962.12`) as `B2` overtakes Mean (`₹473.48` vs `₹432.71`). Under outsider LCV/4% commission stress (`v7_exp5`), `Outsider_Mid_LCV_4pctArh` yields `Safe_EV ₹212.28 / −₹668.22 / −₹842.50` and `Outsider_MinPrice_LCV_4pctArh` drops `Safe_EV` to `₹102.62` (`34.29%` loss, `cond_ES10 −₹1,290.83` vs `B2 ₹33.86 / −₹1,573.13`).
8. **Origin-Report Conditioning & Duplicate/Outlier Hygiene:** Conditioning on a day-`t` origin quote omits `28,420` episodes (`N = 86,054` unconditional yields `Safe_EV` `ES10 −₹336.19` vs `−₹513.75`, and symmetric open-origin `+₹175.19 ES10` in `v7_exp3`). Conflicting duplicate rows (`1,814` test), outlier rows (`369` raw -> `57,521` clean eps: `Safe_EV −₹344.17` `ES10`, `p = 0.001`), `cv28 == 0` rows (`5,274` cand rows: `−₹345.52`), and unrestricted `top_k = None` (`307,242` / `414,880` rows: `−₹344.47` / `−₹324.19`) preserve conclusions cleanly.
9. **Coordinate Collisions, Freight & Grade Proxies (`v7_exp5`):** Raw geocoding matched `59.50%` of corridor rows (`N = 40,263`; `19,362` all-5 exact), but coordinate-uniqueness auditing uncovered `56/171` collided markets (`33/171` falsely labeled `"market_exact"`); `clean_market_query` is fixed in `src/data/build_road_network.py`, and true non-collided exact coverage (`35.23%`, `PureAll5_NonCollided_Exact_N6605`) retains `Safe_EV` `34.56%` disp, `Mean ₹194.97` vs `B2 ₹200.37`, `EpLoss 8.30%` vs `17.38%`, `ES10 −₹231.27` vs `−₹449.14` (`p = 0.001`), and `cond_ES10 −₹497.57` vs `−₹687.18` (`p = 0.019`). Grade-explicit `N = 54,858` retains `−₹329.55 ES10` vs `−₹504.55`.
10. **SUTVA & Fleet Crowding (`v7_exp5`):** In `171` test markets, aggregate capacity/crowding (`5%`/`10%` impact per extra truck) depresses `Safe_EV` Mean to `₹86.06` and `−₹5.31/qtl` (vs `B2 ₹17.26` and `−₹75.37`), while a `k <= 2` truck cap per destination-day cuts dispatch to `31.29%` (`₹207.00` Mean, `ES10 −₹230.30`, `cond_ES10 −₹523.12`).
11. **Daily Modal Aggregation & ML/OR Frontier (`v7_exp4`):** Wholesale modal prices mask intraday lot-level quality dispersion; moreover, full-season `17m` ML (`MeanQ10` `−₹420.57` / `NoSafeEV −₹423.88`, `CQR80 −₹367.09`) and fixed `5m` Split-CQR (`−₹414.44`) achieve stronger `cond_ES10` than zero-parameter `Safe_EV_Sunk_m0` (`−₹447.48`), which serves as a training-free, pocket-calculator complement (`~98.8%` Mean / `~94%` `cond_ES10`).
