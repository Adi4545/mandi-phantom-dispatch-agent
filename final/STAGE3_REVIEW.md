# Independent review of Stage III

**Conditional pass for reporting the retrospective experiment.** The learned loss-severity router can select B2 proposals, request the frozen price forecast, and change a dispatch to a local sale. Replaying this action raises mean modeled payoff over B2 in the six-state panel. The margin comparison is sensitive to the uncertainty calculation, and neither learned router raises mean payoff over B2 in the graded-apple panel. Claims of independent replication, field income gains, or a new learning algorithm are **not ready**.

## What was checked

I inspected the B2 proposal reconstruction, payoff rules, historical feature construction, both router training scripts, forecast invocation, bootstrap implementation, and saved outputs. I independently refit both priority models from the candidate files using the shared B2 proposal extractor. I computed their daily allocations and counterfactual veto gains separately from either evaluation runner. The following results agree with the release. Gains use rupees per quintal averaged over every screened episode, including episodes where B2 chose a local sale.

| Priority rule | Six states, forecasts / vetoes | Six states, gain over B2 | Graded apples, forecasts / vetoes | Graded apples, gain over B2 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Normalized margin | 4,175 / 1,893 | +2.7670 | 22 / 6 | −0.9565 |
| Learned loss severity | 4,175 / 782 | +4.1778 | 22 / 6 | −0.3585 |
| Learned signed tool value | 4,175 / 504 | +2.3296 | 22 / 1 | −0.9794 |

The six-state panel contains 69,526 episodes and 43,183 B2 proposals. The apple panel contains 1,537 episodes and 517 proposals. The action always retains the B2 destination or sells locally. The forecast vetoes a selected proposal when its road-adjusted margin plus its destination reference price times the predicted percentage change (expressed as a fraction) is at most ₹100/qtl. With local incremental payoff zero, the realized policy change for a veto is the negative of the modeled B2 payoff. The random benchmark correctly weights each eligible proposal by its daily quota divided by its daily proposal count. ## Chronology, recourse and interpretation

- The loss-severity learner uses 60,093 B2 proposals from 2024. Every candidate in a training episode had completed its three-day observation window by December 31. Its target is the positive part of a trip's modeled loss. A separate oracle exercise reports how much loss the selected set contains under perfect verification. The executable bridge applies the price forecast to that set and consequently can veto profitable trips.
- The signed-value learner fits 26,566 proposals from January–May 2025. Every chosen route had completed its observation window by May 31. The archived price model uses arrivals through December 2024, so the signed-value labels use subsequent forecast outcomes. Of the 1,921 training vetoes, 981 would avoid a modeled loss and 940 would forgo a modeled gain. A non-veto has zero incremental value. Different training periods, features and model classes prevent attributing a performance difference solely to this target.
- Prediction fields comprise the dispatch-date board, current and historical price/report summaries, identifiers, and planned calendar/road information. The forecast and router input lists exclude realized weather, arrival prices, fallback outcomes and payoff. The latter enter training targets and retrospective scoring. The structural payoff can wait two days for an exact report, then use a distress or diversion rule. Actual farmer actions, transactions, same-day report issue times and tool latency are unobserved. Daily allocation assumes that the day's complete set of proposals is available jointly.
- A shallow signed-value tree creates many equal priorities. At the last daily review slot, selected and excluded proposals shared a score on 300 of 311 six-state review days and 20 of 22 apple review days. Of the selected proposals, 2,253 and 20 respectively came from these partially selected ties. The implemented episode-ID ordering is therefore part of the policy, and replication must retain it. The rankings describe the implemented rule.
- The six-state loss-severity advantage over margin is +₹1.41/qtl. Its 95% percentile interval is +₹0.17 to +₹2.79 with time blocks alone and −₹0.39 to +₹3.56 when resampling origin markets as well. Signed tool value yields −₹0.44 relative to margin (−₹2.39 to +₹1.50). Time and market resampling condition on fitted agents and realized daily allocations. The analysis reused previously examined panels, and the apple quota supplied only 22 forecast rows. These qualifications belong alongside any comparative claim. The incremental contribution is the comparison of routing targets, information budgets and executable interventions on a common dispatch simulation. For a confirmatory result, freeze the complete daily policy including tie-breaking, specify the primary comparator and collect a fresh period with decision-time report availability.
