## 3. Adversarial Attack (`A11` Validity & `A12` Novelty Kill-Tests)

### `A11` Validity Attacks & Verdicts
1. *"Isn't your shortlist still selected on day-$t$ knowledge?"* **Killed in Step 2:** All `candidates_6state.parquet` usage is deleted; Top-5 shortlists are rebuilt from raw quotes strictly $\le t-1$ on BOTH origin and destination, verified by counterfactual day-$\ge t^*$ price corruption tests.
2. *"Are `B2` and `MG`/`RR-Abstain` using unequal quote ages or tuning budgets?"* **Killed in Steps 3 & 5:** Both operate on identical `dest_lag_age in 1..3`, identical Top-5 menu, and identical validation-calibrated dispatch (`~70%`), plus `RR_Abstain_m0` (`m=0`) needs zero arbitrary cutoff tuning.
3. *"What if cluster dependence spans destination markets, not origins?"* **Tested:** Every `H_A10_1..4` holds at `p_holm = 0.0020` under BOTH `origin_x_2week` (173 clusters × 23 blocks) and `b2dest_or_origin_x_2week` (336 clusters × 23 blocks).
4. *"Can you claim conformal guarantees or farmer income gains?"* **Explicitly rejected (`R7`):** We removed all conformal theorem claims and state clearly that payoffs come from an assumed freight/spoilage/settlement formula, not farmer cash receipts.

### `A12` Novelty Kill-Attempt & Surviving Claim
*Attack (`A12`):* *"Mixing an open-market gain with a worst-case missing-outcome bound weighted by empirical missingness probability is literally textbook Bayesian/Chow decision theory with Manski bounds applied to Baulch spatial arbitrage. Nothing mathematically new exists!"*  
*Defense (`A10`/`A9`):* `A12` is **100% right that the underlying expected-value decision rule is elementary algebra — and we make zero claim to a new theorem or ML architecture.** What DOES survive `A12` in applied agricultural market systems is **empirical + operational**, not a new theorem: prior Indian mandi systems (Ma et al. 2019; Madaan et al. 2019; Biradar & Divyashree 2026) impute missing prices to minimize RMSE, while Stage I (`MandiGuard`) filtered thin yards via ad-hoc hard cutoffs (`rep14 >= 0.50`, `dow8 >= 0.35`, `margin=200/500`) that left matched `ES10` unimproved (`p_holm = 0.589`). Replacing imputation and hard ad-hoc gates with a **strictly lagged reporting-reliability-weighted missing-quote loss bound (`RR-Abstain`)** yields a **+₹64.54/qtl matched-dispatch `ES10` gain over age-matched road-cost baselines and a +₹52.06/qtl `ES10` gain over hard-gated `MG` (`p_holm = 0.0020`, holding across all three non-apple crops and down to zero missing penalty)** while reducing algorithmic complexity to a single calculator equation.

### Single Defensible Novelty Sentence (Surviving `A12`)
> **"In a strictly causal (`<= t-1`), sequential-settlement retrospective evaluation across 58,676 Indian wholesale dispatch episodes (4 crops, 6 states), weighting each corridor's lagged cost-adjusted spread by its prior variety-reporting reliability against an explicit per-quintal non-quote loss bound (`RR-Abstain`) replaces point-price imputation and hard activity thresholds with a single calculator-verifiable rule that improves matched-dispatch worst-10% shortfall (`ES10` by +₹64.54/qtl over age-matched road dispatch and +₹52.06/qtl over hard-gated filtering, Holm `p = 0.0020`) across non-apple staples — though validation remains exploratory wrt calendar exposure and graded-apple volatility remains unresolved."**
