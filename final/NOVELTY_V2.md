## Core Scientific Thesis & Methodological Humility
We explicitly distinguish **problem/evaluation novelty** from **methodological novelty**: `Safe_EV_Sunk_m0`, `Safe_EV_Phys_m0`, and `RT_2F_Min_m0` do **not** claim a new statistical theorem, conformal bound, or stochastic optimization algorithm (mean-variance / tail-risk dispatch and $1/N$-style heuristic benchmarks exist in DeMiguel et al. 2009, Gigerenzer & Gaissmaier 2011, and Vargas, Klapp & Mac Cawley 2026). Instead, MandiGuard makes an **applied, empirical, and evaluation contribution**: diagnosing how day-$0$ wholesale commodity non-reporting under sequential dispatch creates a severe, state- and weekday-structured censoring trap on public AGMARKNET data, and showing honestly where zero-tuned-parameter domain heuristics sit on the ML/OR Pareto frontier (`v7_exp4`). Retiring any claim that zero-parameter rules beat all ML, we show earlier ML lags stemmed from `5m` starvation (`Ridge`/`HGBR`), quadratic $\text{INR}^2$ penalties (`OR_Vargas` `−₹511.42`), and homoscedastic CQR (`0` switches); fixed heteroscedastic Split-CQR (`MG_Intact_matched −₹414.44`) and full-season `17m` ML (`MeanQ10 −₹420.57` / `NoSafeEV −₹423.88`, `CQR80 −₹367.09`) achieve stronger `cond_ES10`, while zero-parameter `Safe_EV_Sunk_m0` (`0` fitted params, `9` fixed structural choices incl. `z = 1.0`) captures `~98.8%` of `17m MeanQ10` Mean and `~94%` of `cond_ES10` tail protection with zero training history and pocket-calculator portability.

We likewise do **not** construct a strawman claiming prior agricultural economists believed wholesale gaps are strictly `MAR`. Rather, price-transmission and market-information studies focus predominantly on spatial integration on observed price series or portal-level price effects, leaving unmeasured the episode-level arrival payoff distribution when per-shipment physical turn-backs interact with missing day-$0$ quotes.
