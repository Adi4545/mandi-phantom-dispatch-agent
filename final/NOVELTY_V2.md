## Core Scientific Thesis & Methodological Humility
We explicitly distinguish **problem/evaluation novelty** from **methodological novelty**: `Safe_EV_Sunk_m0`, `Safe_EV_Phys_m0`, and `RT_2F_Min_m0` do **not** claim a new statistical theorem, conformal bound, or stochastic optimization algorithm (mean-variance / tail-risk dispatch and $1/N$-style heuristic benchmarks exist in DeMiguel et al. 2009, Gigerenzer & Gaissmaier 2011, and Vargas, Klapp & Mac Cawley 2026). Instead, MandiGuard makes an **applied, empirical, and evaluation contribution**: diagnosing how day-$0$ wholesale commodity non-reporting under sequential dispatch creates a severe, state- and weekday-structured censoring trap on public AGMARKNET data, and showing that zero-tuned-parameter domain triage heuristics outperform comparable-coverage retrained ML/OR benchmarks on conditional tail risk (`cond_ES10`) across lookahead-free (`171`), archival (`166` Clean `2018–2023`), and adversarial stress regimes (while noting ultra-conservative `ML_HGBR_Q20_m0` halves coverage to `32.83%`).

We likewise do **not** construct a strawman claiming prior agricultural economists believed wholesale gaps are strictly `MAR`. Rather, price-transmission and market-information studies focus predominantly on spatial integration on observed price series or portal-level price effects, leaving unmeasured the episode-level arrival payoff distribution when per-shipment physical turn-backs interact with missing day-$0$ quotes.
