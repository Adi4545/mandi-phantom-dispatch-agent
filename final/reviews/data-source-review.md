# What the mandi records establish

**Data and agricultural-economics review, 21 November 2026.** Baseline commit `d20a0cb25827b73688e4ced19948ac5c01e49f6b`. This review used the repository's cached source tables, the government manuals and survey tables below, and an independently written recount. The direct portal export and bulk annual mirror files were not independently downloaded. Agreement means agreement between archived records. A historical truck journey, sale, or loss would require separate evidence.

`src/data/extract_agmarknet_multistate.py::build_active_panel` ranks markets using training-year data but applies an 80-day eligibility threshold to the complete observation window. Seven retained markets fail an 80-day training-only threshold. The alternative defines a different cohort. Freeze eligibility in training and rerun before making a forward-selection claim. The data cleaning function fills empty varieties and grades and tests minimum and maximum ordering without enforcing minimum ≤ modal ≤ maximum. The six-state builder maps crop-name, “Other”, and “FAQ” varieties to “Standard” and collapses grades before comparing prices. An exact match in this analysis is a match under those transformations. ### What a missing row means

The DMI operator manual allows local choice of commodities and varieties, previous-date entries, corrections and an explicit nil-transaction notification with a reason. Weekly holidays, seasonal selection, delayed upload, a different variety, trading outside the reporting channel, or actual absence of trading are possible explanations. The archive alone does not identify which occurred. Both the date on the record and its first public availability matter. The documented freeze permits weekend corrections through Monday night. A decision allegedly made that morning needs portal vintages and an explicit dispatch clock. [DMI data entry SOP, §§3.2–3.5 and 5.1–5.7](https://www.agmarknet.gov.in/DailyDataEntry/Docs/SOP_DEO.pdf)

`build_dispatch_episodes.py` creates `any_open_mat` after limiting its input to four agricultural products, or apple alone in the grade study. In the official 18 Oct 2023 XML, five core markets reported exclusively other crops. They include Arasikere and Raichur. All five disappear when that day's official rows are filtered to the four study crops. The `closed_yard_divert` branch checks the filtered panel at arrival day and the absence of a matching variety over a three-day window. Its name cannot establish three days of yard closure. A defensible label is “no study-crop report at arrival and no matching quote within three days.” A different branch assigns a distress discount when another variety has a quote. Neither branch follows a truck.

DMI defines modal price using daily trading activity. It supplies a market summary. The six-state screen further uses an unweighted median across reported varieties and grades which can be carried forward seven days. A scored sale combines a selected quote with a hypothetical price impact, quality and quantity assumption. Receipt, payment settlement, freight bill, observed buyer demand, and a local-sale counterfactual would be necessary to identify a farmer's incremental income. [DMI data entry SOP, §3.3](https://www.agmarknet.gov.in/DailyDataEntry/Docs/SOP_DEO.pdf)

## Routes, weather and decision time

| Input | Evidence and consequence |
|---|---|
| Route distances | The cache has 8,147 intrastate directional routes, 15–500 km. Of 220 endpoint coordinates, the code labels 150 as exact and 70 as district centroids. In 4,592 corridors at least one endpoint is a centroid. “Exact” denotes a geocoder hit within its acceptance criteria, including a 110 km district check. No physical yard survey verifies these coordinates. A driving-profile fastest-route distance is a model output. Applying truck time multipliers and a four-hour loading buffer, and dropping co-located centroid pairs, changes eligibility and modeled arrival dates. Vehicle-specific access, seasonal road closures, tolls, loading delays and historical network vintages are unverified. [OSRM Table service](https://project-osrm.org/docs/v26.6.1/http.html) |
| Same-day ERA5 | For 43 of 145 grid cells the fitted weather proxy includes that day's realized maximum temperature, rainfall and humidity. Copernicus documents about five days before preliminary availability and possible revision months later. This use as a day-of-decision feature contradicts the claimed information cutoff. Future weather can be used to *score* a hypothetical trip ex post. [Copernicus ERA5 data documentation](https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/datasets/reanalysis-era5-single-levels-timeseries) |
| Archived “forecast” cells | All 99,858 labeled NWP rows agree exactly with the realized rainfall **and** humidity columns, including 43,408 positive-rainfall rows. The archive endpoint stitches the first hours of successive runs. Valid date alone cannot establish that a whole daily aggregate was issued when a farmer was choosing a destination. The matching values do not identify the cause. Archive a single forecast run, publication timestamp, lead, local decision time and input vintage, or omit the feature. [Open-Meteo historical forecast product](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/historical-forecast-api) · [Single-run endpoint](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/single-runs-api) |
| NASA POWER | The 2017–2023 manifest identifies the daily point product, while the cache uses column names beginning with `era5_`. POWER documents its own reanalysis/assimilation sources and a 2–3 day meteorology latency. Preserve source-specific names and treat contemporaneous POWER values as ex post weather. A grid estimate is a proxy for temperatures inside a vehicle. [NASA source methodology](https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/methodology/data/sources_meteorology/) · [NASA latency](https://power.larc.nasa.gov/docs/faqs/data/) |

## Source access and reuse

The mirror identifies an MIT licence on its dataset card. The OGD platform identifies the government provider and requires attribution under the Government Open Data License–India, subject to its exclusions. Maintain per-file source, retrieval time, attribution, upstream licence and the distinction between mirror processing and original government content. A mirror label cannot establish permissions for every original record. The repository’s annual URLs point to mutable `main`; the published extractor records SHA-256 digests after download. The upstream file-detail SHA-256 values independently matched the stored manifest for 2017 and 2021–2026 (including all three source files for the modern panel). The web archive did not return pointers for 2018–2020. These comparisons establish file identity with the mirror under the recorded hashes. Eight cached government-document hashes matched their manifest. That verifies preservation of those files only. No independent byte-for-byte download of the full annual source CSVs or per-query HP export log was obtained in this review. [OGD terms](https://www.data.gov.in/terms-of-use) · [GODL terms](https://data.gov.in/Godl) · [Mirror card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Arunvithyasegar/AGMARK_Commodity_Pricing_Variety_Wise_Daily_Market_Prices_Data_Commodity_2022_2025)

Credit OpenStreetMap contributors and identify ODbL where OSM material is redistributed. Review the actual derived artifact under the relevant licence. Open-Meteo distinguishes attribution of weather data from terms and limits of its hosted API. For each product keep its request parameters, vintage, provider credits and access conditions alongside its hash. [OpenStreetMap terms](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) · [Open-Meteo terms](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms)

## Reproduction and verdict

From the research repository, run `python scripts/audit_data_sources.py .` using pandas and pyarrow, with the repository at the baseline commit above. The script reads cached inputs and emits JSON to stdout. Compare with the saved `final/data-source-audit.json`. The two joins compare distinct keys and price triplets and count unmatched records in both directions. No historical record was modified.

**Conditional for retrospective portal-report analysis. Not ready for claims of point-in-time forecast performance, market closure, transaction-level profitability or farmer welfare.** A prospective evaluation would freeze a timestamped policy and as-of inputs, observe eligible lots and alternatives including stay-local, collect settlement records, buyer offers, grade, quantity, travel histories, invoices and salvage outcomes, and predefine how treatment/control markets and missing receipts enter the comparison. An untouched retrospective period could test reporting and scenario scores with honest temporal separation. Only real dispatch and comparator evidence can evaluate actual income effects.
