# Review 2 — novelty

Role: literature examiner. Pass 2 of 3, same agent, after the validity pass. Not independent.

## Findings

The economic object — a price gap that cannot be traded — is old. Jensen (2007), Sekhar (2012), and the mandi forecasting papers already sit on this ground. “Phantom spatial arbitrage” should be introduced as a label, and the manuscript now does that.

The selective-labels citation is an analogy. Lakkaraju et al. (2017) concern labels hidden by the decision. Here many labels are missing because the portal has no row. That distinction belongs in the defense.

Conformal risk control is not a contribution of this repo. The implementation violates the theorem’s exchangeability and fixed-denominator conditions. After the edit, the paper calls the rule an empirical gate. That is the only accurate description.

Biradar’s byline and URL, the Mandi Cast URL, and the Deka DOI were wrong or unresolved in the form committed on `main`. They are corrected on this branch. They are minor applied systems papers, not the foundation of the result. Mandi Cast’s 3.29% MAPE is reported by its authors on synthetic data; it should not be used as a field benchmark.

What remains that a thesis can own: a time-respecting multi-state evaluation in which a fresh-price road rule is the serious baseline, H2 fails, non-quote penalties are varied, and a later risk gate is labeled post-hoc.

## Verdict

Novelty is real only at that narrow scope. A claim of a new phenomenon, a new conformal theorem, or an LLM agent would not survive review.
