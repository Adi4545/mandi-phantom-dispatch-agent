# Independent adversarial review of MandiGuard

**Verdict on the original manuscript: NOT READY.** The HP apple baseline comparisons are computationally reproducible under the published simulator. The original physiological, market-closure, operational forecast, distribution-free risk, and welfare interpretations exceed the evidence. A narrower paper about retrospective decisions can survive. This review applies to historical main `d20a0cb` and identifies changes inherited from proposed finalization `b399358` explicitly. It records objections before subsequent repairs. A later repair needs its own verification record.

The reviewer worked separately from the novelty investigator and from the person integrating the revision. Inspected Git history, implementation, source papers and data. Independently implemented the HP payoff and two baseline selectors. The v1 rerun reused the released model and policy evaluator. Commands, input hashes, counts and full-precision results are available in [the check script](../../scripts/audit_weather_and_apple.py) and [the machine-readable results](../weather-and-apple-checks.json). Other six-state numerical comparisons quoted below are inspections of the historical CSVs unless expressly described as recomputed.

| A9-04. Conditional risk guarantee. Critical for guarantee claims | The grid search computes a selection-dependent ratio using dispatches as its denominator. Both clear a ₹50 hurdle. If the second route incurs a modeled loss and the first does not, episode loss rises when λ increases. In Exp 17 the historical trip-loss rate is 9.15% on validation, 10.48% on the six-state test period and 21.08% on HP apples, against 9.5% validation target. Exceedance alone is not a test of the theorem. | Specify validation heuristic, dispatch frequency, conditional and unconditional loss and observed test rates. Thm 1 needs exchangeable bounded monotone losses with an appropriate fixed calibration sample. The assumptions and risk functional here do not match. The original manuscript acknowledges some caveats in §2 but uses stronger language elsewhere. The previous branch corrected the zero-dispatch convention. Verify infeasible-grid handling as well, since the historical default returns the last multiplier plus placeholder mean. [Angelopoulos et al., equation 4 and theorem 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2208.02814) |

## Round 2. Originality challenge

Baulch models intermarket price differentials relative to transfer costs and their implied no-trade regions. His experiments use simulated data and Philippine wholesale rice data. This predates naming a gross spread that expenses erase. MandiGuard adds an explicit hypothetical decision at an arrival date and exact-variety quote completeness in this panel. Earlier work alone does not establish whether the specific benchmark exists elsewhere. [Baulch, *American Journal of Agricultural Economics*, 1997, pp. 477–487](https://citeseerx.ist.psu.edu/document?doi=4a4f58ab2df2f6d6ab1d4315274235c8d4a2efc7&repid=rep1&type=pdf)

Fafchamps and Minten explicitly model farmers choosing between local and distant markets under transport costs. Their Maharashtra information-service experiment involved 933 farmers in 100 villages. Take-up and some spatial decisions changed, while average farmer price effects were statistically inconclusive. A portal-based simulator answers a materially narrower question. Studies on actual farmer outcomes belong in the manuscript alongside forecasting benchmarks. [Author manuscript, model and Tables 12–13](https://web.stanford.edu/~fafchamp/rml.pdf)

The most defensible incremental contribution is a reproducible Indian exact-variety decision evaluation with a native sell-local counterfactual and explicit reporting-gap settlement scenarios. The term “phantom” needs an operational definition conditioned on the declared rule. Claims of a new market phenomenon, new risk-control theorem, or verified farmer benefit fail review. The case for a stand-alone new algorithm remains unsupported.

## Round 3. Replication and communication

The script checks original candidate payoffs against an independent vectorized reconstruction before evaluating the two independently implemented baseline rules. V1's archived tree and action implementation are reused. This rerun needs five compact local inputs plus the cleaned six-state archive used for the panel selection check. **Disposition.** A supervisor can assess the narrow simulation and the evidence behind the HP means. Application to actual trading and claims of generalizable Stage II superiority require new evidence. A submission manuscript must resolve or explicitly bound A9-01 through A9-10 and synchronize all teaching materials with the corrected definitions.
