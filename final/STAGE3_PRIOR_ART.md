# Stage III prior art and claim limits

**Independent novelty assessment, 21 November 2026.** Budgeting verification of the fresh-road heuristic (B2) is an application of costly information acquisition and selective deferral. Learning when to accept B2 instead of selling locally is a binary policy-learning application. The contribution worth testing is whether either decision improves a specified mandi replay objective over a simple, equally informed rule at the same budget. A new algorithm, an observed benefit of calling a market, and an improvement in farmer income each require evidence beyond such a replay.

## What the two experiments can establish

**Budgeted verification.** Let Y be B2’s scenario payoff relative to selling locally, X the information available before review, and c the cost per review in the same units as Y. If unchecked routes follow B2 and a hypothetical perfect checker vetoes every loss, reviewing a route is worth E[max(−Y,0) | X] − c. Ranking by loss probability is optimal for the *count* of corrected losses under equal review costs and perfect detection. Rupee losses vary, so this ranking can differ from the ranking that prevents the most rupees of loss. Report both loss capture and the share of total modeled loss amount captured. For an imperfect checker with a signal Z, the expected post-check payoff includes revenue foregone when a profitable route is incorrectly blocked. The perfect-check curve is a scenario ceiling. It cannot estimate actual verification value.

Compare equal-capacity random review and an ex ante simple score such as low B2 margin or sparse prior reporting. Include zero review and an oracle ordering for scale. Count B2’s own checks separately from the additional verification budget. A daily top-k queue assumes the day's candidates are jointly available before departure. A single threshold frozen on validation supports sequential arrivals, with potentially different realized review fractions. Identify the queue assumption used.

**Learned B2 versus local.** Holding the B2 route fixed makes the action space interpretable. For a risk-neutral modeled net-return objective, take B2 when E[Y | X] exceeds zero. A cutoff on P(Y < 0 | X) targets a different quantity and can reject rare, valuable opportunities. Report paired mean gain per eligible episode, dispatch rate, loss rate among dispatches, and the risk of rare severe losses. Compare to B2, always local, and a validation-tuned margin threshold. Train only on matured scenario payoffs and use inputs available before the decision. A rule that often sells locally can lower loss incidence while sacrificing mean payoff. Any estimate on previously inspected test periods remains exploratory.

**Priority verdict.** A limited search found close precedents for both mechanisms. It does not establish global first use of their conjunction in mandi dispatch. Use a descriptive name such as “budgeted verification of B2” or “learned B2 acceptance.” If the experiments show no durable advantage over the margin or sparsity heuristic, report that result as the empirical finding.
