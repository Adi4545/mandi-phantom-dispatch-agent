Part 3: Related Work (49 Citations), Limitations, Novelty Overlap & Viva Defense Bank

5. Related Work and Surviving Applied Contribution

Five research streams frame our audit and design choices:

  1. Spatial Arbitrage, Market Power, and Agricultural Information Services: Parity-bound models show that gross spreads below transfer costs yield zero arbitrage [baulch1997transfer], and empirical studies of Indian wholesale integration document persistent spatial wedges driven by transport frictions, market density, and local intermediary power [sekhar2012agricultural, chatterjee2016six, negi2018farmers, mahajan2021covid, chatterjee2023market]. While mobile price discovery reduced fish waste in Kerala [jensen2007digital] and raised soybean prices near hubs [goyal2010information], randomized SMS and market-information trials show muted or heterogeneous gains when bargaining power, credit ties, or execution frictions bind [fafchamps2012sms, courtois2015farmer, mitra2018asymmetric], whereas unified e-auction platforms raise realized prices when transaction execution is reliable [levi2020impact, levi2025improving].
  2. Agricultural Price Forecasting and Market Intelligence Systems: Applied computing systems predict wholesale mandi prices via time-series, collaborative filtering, or deep learning [madaan2019price, ma2019price, guo2020mandi], study farmer-facing market intelligence workflows [ziegler2019fresh, ladhar2023ai], or reconstruct missing portal rows using regional factor models [deka2026missing]. Those studies optimize price-prediction RMSE or imputation error rather than evaluating sequential non-reporting arrival execution and worst-decile dispatch shortfall under strict <= t - 1 timing.
  3. Decision-Focused Learning and Selective Abstention: Decision-focused learning and smart predict-then-optimize frameworks align scoring directly with downstream operational loss [ban2019bigdata, elmachtoub2022smart, mandi2024dfl], while reject-option and selective-prediction theory formalizes abstention under asymmetric error costs [chow1970optimum, el_yaniv2010selective, geifman2017selective, franc2023optimal]. Our contribution demonstrates empirically that in thin agricultural portals, simple zero-parameter expected-value ratios using weekday reporting reliability and historical volatility outperform both hard activity thresholds and grid-tuned abstention penalties.
  4. Missing-Data Mechanisms, Partial Identification, and Tail Risk: Rubin's missing-data taxonomy [rubin1976inference], Manski's nonparametric bounds [manski1990bounds, horowitz2000nonparametric], selective-label evaluation [lakkaraju2017selective], and minimax policy learning under unobserved confounding [kallus2021minimax] motivate bracketing unobserved blank arrival rows rather than treating missingness as purely closed or purely open. Tail risk is measured via coherent expected shortfall [rockafellar2000cvar].
  5. Conformal Risk Control and Non-Exchangeable Inference: Distribution-free conformal prediction, risk control, and conformal decision theory [angelopoulos2023conformal, barber2023beyond, jin2023selection, angelopoulos2024conformal, lekeufack2024conformal] offer finite-sample guarantees under exchangeability or bounded shift, but fail to hold out of the box under action-dependent selective arrival denominators and correlated commodity regimes—explaining why we claim zero conformal guarantees and evaluate via two-way cluster bootstraps with finite-sample Holm corrections [phipson2010permutation, holm1979simple].

Minimal Surviving Applied Contribution: Claiming no new mathematical theorem or neural architecture, this study establishes in a strictly <= t - 1, sequentially settled retrospective audit (N = 58,676, corroborated on a fresh N = 57,634 2024-only market rebuild, realized ERA5 destination arrival-weather settlement, and N = 88,014 unconditional origins) that replacing black-box trees, hard activity cutoffs, and tuned penalties with zero-hyperparameter expected-value ratios—Safe_EV_Sunk_m0 (pdow G1SD > F), settlement-matched Safe_EV_Phys_m0 (1.5F), and Class-8 RT_2F_Min_m0 (pmin G > 2F)—eliminates tuning parameters while improving worst-10% ES10 over equal-age road dispatch (B2_age_matched) by +₹179.24 to +₹201.55/qtl across the four closed-yard panels (p_holm_2s = 0.0024 at R = 5,000), with positive 95% ES10 CIs vs B2 in every state and crop. Against tuned RR_Abstain_matched, validation-matched Safe_EV_Sunk_matched improves ES10 by +₹94.31/qtl (p_holm_2s = 0.0024–0.0040, while threshold-free Safe_EV_Sunk_m0 gains +₹116.63/qtl overall at lower 63.27% vs 69.02% coverage, trails RR by −₹9.77/qtl on aggregate mean, and trails RR by −₹16.60/qtl on Potato ES10: −₹243.97 vs −₹227.37).

6. Explicit Limitations and Non-Claims

Eight explicit boundaries govern interpretation and deployment:

  1. Modeled Payoffs ≠ Farmer Cash Income: AGMARKNET records contain no physical truck freight receipts, weighment shrinkage, or auction patti slips; freight, loading (₹35), detention (₹25), and Q10 decay are survey-anchored approximations (NITI Aayog, NABCONS, GoHP), not audited household income.
  2. A Missing Portal Quote ≠ A Physically Closed Yard: Blank arrival rows mean no public record was uploaded, not necessarily a locked gate. Under the upper Open-MAR counterfactual, B2 earns +₹102.58/qtl (+₹100.57 under ERA5) higher mean than Safe_EV_Sunk_m0, with exact break-even occurring when silent trips average +₹328.37/qtl above turn-back (23.79% Open-MAR share); even at Open-MAR, Safe_EV_Sunk_m0 retains +₹190.00/qtl (+₹196.38 ERA5) ES10 protection and lower episode losses.
  3. Alert-Triage Scope Prior to Live Phone Confirmation: Because a 30-second phone call to a destination arhtiya resolves yard closure before loading—leaving pure open-market price risk where B2 and Safe_EV_Sunk_m0 yield close open-auction means (₹930.65 vs ₹924.65) while Safe_EV halves open ES10 (−₹307.70 vs −₹623.62)—these rules serve as an upstream 6 a.m. alert filter, never a replacement for trader verification.
  4. Exploratory Calendar Exposure (Gate J = BLOCKED): Bulk AGMARKNET archives stop at 2026-04-13 (live endpoints expose only rolling 7-day snapshots), so PREREG_A10.md and PREREG_A18.md are locked exploratory follow-up protocols on a previously exposed 2025–2026 calendar, not prospective clinical-style trials.
  5. Day-t Origin Conditioning Disclosed: Primary tables condition on day-t origin uploads (58,676 of 88,014 unconditional shortlisted test episodes); full unconditional origin results (Safe_EV_Sunk_m0 ES10 −₹335.04 vs −₹515.93) confirm qualitative invariance.
  6. Residual Graded-Apple and Tomato Volatility: Although Safe_EV_Sunk_m0 narrows HP graded-apple ES10 from −₹2,392.12 to −₹1,454.86, 41.38% of dispatched apple trips still lose money; on Tomato, Safe_EV_Sunk_m0 leaves mean flat (₹461.42 vs ₹462.27) while improving ES10 by +₹195.65/qtl.
  7. District Centroids, Conflicting Duplicates, and Partial Equilibrium: 41.71% of shortlisted test rows touch district-centroid coordinates and 1,819 of 58,676 test episodes (3,096 of 95,074 combined validation and test episodes) touch conflicting duplicate records (verified stable on exact-geocode N = 40,690 and non-conflicting N = 56,857 test subsets); mass simultaneous FPO dispatch could also move thin destination prices, an equilibrium effect not modeled here.
  8. Zero-Parameter Rules ≠ Conformal Coverage Guarantees: No distribution-free coverage guarantee is claimed (Stage I H2 remained inconclusive at +₹6.13/qtl, p_holm = 0.2699).

7. Conclusion

Attractive online mandi price spreads lose money nearly half the time (45.21%) under a strict <= t - 1 chronological firewall and non-anticipative sequential arrival settlement. Stripping away black-box tree pickles, weather leakage, conformal overclaims, and tuned penalty grids leaves three zero-hyperparameter expected-value rules—Safe_EV_Sunk_m0 (pdow G1SD > F), Safe_EV_Phys_m0 (1.5F), and Class-8 RT_2F_Min_m0 (pmin G > 2F)—that any FPO coordinator can compute on a pocket calculator. Across N = 58,676 test episodes, a fresh N = 57,634 2024-only market rebuild, and realized ERA5 destination arrival-weather settlement, these rules improve worst-10% ES10 over B2_age_matched by +₹179.24 to +₹201.55/qtl (p_holm_2s = 0.0024 at R = 5,000), corroborated by +₹180.89/qtl on the N = 88,014 unconditional origin panel and strictly positive R = 1,000 95% ES10 CIs vs B2 across all six states and all five crop slices—providing an auditable, transparently bounded triage benchmark ready for prospective FPO field testing.

Q1. What is the single problem and why did naive portal arbitrage fail so often?

An online AGMARKNET spread looks like instant profit (> ₹100/qtl), yet across 58,676 lagged test episodes (2025-06-01..2026-04-08), blindly chasing top spreads (B1_lagged_screen, 100.00% dispatch) loses money on 45.21% of days (mean ₹397.87/qtl, exact ES10 −₹641.26/qtl). Three physical frictions—road freight (₹35 + 0.45 * hp * km), destination post-spoilage APMC seller fees (0.5%–1.5%), and 28.98% (17,005 trips) encountering zero uploaded trade on arrival—turn paper spreads into turn-back losses under sequential non-anticipative settlement.

Q4. Explain exact ES10 and what cluster-key bug your own adversarial review caught (A17 #1).

Unweighted below-quantile averages (the legacy truncated quantile-mean estimator) broke whenever ₹0 abstentions tied at the 10% boundary. Exact ES10 sorts all N episodes worst-first (₹0 on abstentions), sets fractional mass m = 0.10 N = k + f, sums the worst k observations plus fraction f of (k + 1), and divides by m. On clustering: earlier runs forgot State + "|" on bare dest_market, splitting physical markets into 336 duplicate strings instead of 172. Fixing State + "|" and adding policy-specific destination clustering yields exact 173, 172, and 171–173 clusters (1-week 45, 2-week 23, and 4-week 12 calendar blocks) and uses strict two-sided Holm-Bonferroni at R = 5,000 (p_holm_2s = 0.0024 for tail/loss/RT-mean; 0.0028–0.0436 for Safe_EV mean vs B2)—all primary hypotheses still hold cleanly across all 3 schemes.
