Part 3: Related Work, Novelty Defense, Limitations & Viva Bank

5. Related Work and Surviving Novelty Claim

Prior agricultural economics literature established that spreads below transfer costs yield no arbitrage [baulch1997transfer] and that raw price SMS blasts frequently underdeliver when transport or intermediary barriers bind [fafchamps2012sms, mitra2018asymmetric, mahajan2021covid], whereas real-time phone coordination reduces price waste [jensen2007digital]. Applied agricultural CS studies forecast portal prices [madaan2019price, guo2020mandi] or impute blank rows via matrix/factor models [ma2019price, deka2026missing], minimizing forecast RMSE without pricing sequential non-reporting arrival risk or tail expected shortfall. Reject-option classification [chow1970optimum, franc2023optimal], partial identification bounds [manski1990bounds, horowitz2000nonparametric, kallus2021minimax], and cost-sensitive operational optimization [ban2019bigdata] provide the underlying mathematical building blocks, while conformal selection/CRC methods [jin2023selection, angelopoulos2024conformal] require exchangeable data and action-independent risk denominators absent here.

Minimal Surviving Applied Contribution (A18 + A12): Claiming zero new mathematical theorems or neural architectures, this study proves in a leak-free (<= t - 1), sequentially settled retrospective audit (N = 58,676, plus N = 88,014 unconditional origin robustness) that replacing black-box trees, hard activity cutoffs, and tuned penalties with two zero-hyperparameter expected-value rules—weekday-reliability-weighted 1-SD spread minus sunk freight (Safe_EV_Sunk_m0, pdow G1SD > F) and round-trip spread rule (RT_2F_Min_m0, pmin G > 2F)—eliminates tuning parameters while cutting ES10 losses by +₹181.17/qtl over equal-age road dispatch (p_holm_2s = 0.0040) and +₹116.63/qtl over tuned RR-Abstain across every state and crop.

6. Explicit Limitations and Non-Claims (R6 & R7)

Eight binding boundaries govern interpretation and deployment:

  1. Modeled Payoffs ≠ Farmer Cash Income (R7): Zero physical freight bills, weighment losses, or mandi auction patti slips exist in AGMARKNET; freight, loading (₹35), detention (₹25), and Q10 decay are survey-anchored approximations (NITI Aayog, NABCONS, GoHP), not household cash profits.
  2. A Missing Portal Quote ≠ A Locked Yard (R7): Blank arrival rows show no uploaded record, not physical closure. Under upper-bound Open-MAR upload omissions, B2 earns +₹102.58/qtl higher mean than Safe_EV_Sunk_m0, though Safe_EV still cuts losses (4.78% vs 7.08%) and boosts ES10 (+₹190.00).
  3. Alert-Triage Scope Prior to Phone Confirmation (A17 #10): Because a 30-second phone call to a destination arhtiya can resolve whether a yard is trading before departure—leaving pure open-market price risk where B2 and Safe_EV_Sunk_m0 earn similar open-auction means (₹930.65 vs ₹924.65, though Safe_EV cuts open ES10 in half)—our rules act as an upstream portal-alert filter, not a substitute for human trader confirmation.
  4. Exploratory Calendar Exposure (Gate J = BLOCKED): Bulk official archives end 2026-04-13 (api.agmarknet.gov.in exposes only a 7-day rolling modal snippet), so PREREG_A10.md and PREREG_A18.md are locked exploratory follow-up protocols on a previously exposed 2025–2026 calendar, not untouched prospective trials.
  5. Day-t Origin Conditioning Disclosed: Primary tables condition on day-t origin publication (58,676 of 88,014 unconditional shortlisted test episodes); we report the full 88,014-episode unconditional panel cleanly alongside primary results (Safe_EV_Sunk_m0 ES10 −₹335.04 vs −₹515.93).
  6. Residual Graded-Apple & Tomato Volatility (R6): Although Safe_EV_Sunk_m0 cuts HP Apple ES10 from −₹2,392.12 to −₹1,454.86, 41.38% of dispatched apple trips still lose money; on Tomato, Safe_EV_Sunk_m0 leaves mean flat (₹461.42 vs ₹462.27) while slashing tail risk (+₹195.65 ES10).
  7. District Centroids, Conflicts, & Partial Equilibrium: 41.71% of rows touch district-centroid coordinates and 3,096 episodes touch conflicting duplicates (verified stable when restricted to exact geocodes and clean keys); mass simultaneous FPO coordination would also induce price slippage not modeled here.
  8. Validation Calibration / Zero-Param Heuristic ≠ Conformal Guarantee (R7): Zero conformal coverage guarantees are claimed (Stage I H2 inconclusive at +₹6.13, p_holm = 0.2699).

Q1. What is the single problem and why did naive portal arbitrage fail so often?

An online AGMARKNET spread looks like instant profit (> ₹100/qtl), yet across 58,676 lagged test episodes (2025-06-01..2026-04-08), blindly chasing top spreads (B1_lagged_screen, 100.00% dispatch) loses money on 45.21% of days (mean ₹397.87/qtl, exact ES10 −₹641.26/qtl). Three physical frictions—road freight (₹35 + 0.45 * hp * km), destination post-spoilage APMC seller fees (0.5%–1.5%), and 28.98% (17,005 trips) encountering zero uploaded trade on arrival—turn paper spreads into turn-back losses under sequential non-anticipative settlement.

Q2. Why did you move beyond tuned RR-Abstain (kappa*=5.0) to zero-parameter Safe_EV_Sunk_m0 & RT_2F_Min_m0?

Adversarial review (A17 + A18) uncovered three clean flaws in RR-Abstain:

  1. kappa* = 5.0 hit the grid wall: Extending the grid to 20.0 pushed the matched threshold down to −₹328.99/qtl (showing kappa = 5.0 worked as a disguised cutoff shift rather than a physical 1.5× return factor).
  2. rep28 over-predicted Sunday closings: Mandis closed regularly on Sundays still scored 0.75 under a 28-day rolling average, inflating 0.5*rep28 + 0.5*dow8. Using weekday-only dow8 ((k+1)/10 over the last 8 same-weekdays) fixed Sunday prediction directly.
  3. Price swings (cv28) were ignored: High lagged prices regress downward on arrival (Apple / Tomato). Subtracting 1 standard deviation (dest_price_cv_28d) gives Safe_EV_Sunk_m0 (pdow· G1SD - F > 0 — 0 free params, cuts ES10 from −₹525.25 to −₹344.09/qtl, +₹181.17 vs B2, p_holm_2s = 0.0040 on all 3 clustering schemes, positive in all 6 states and HP apples). Dropping cv28 altogether leaves Class-8 RT_2F_Min_m0 (pmin· G > 2F — 0 free params, ₹478.38/qtl mean, +₹44.82 vs B2, p_holm_2s = 0.0040).

Q3. Why would an FPO use your math instead of just calling a trader on the phone (A17 #10)?

It doesn't replace the phone call—it prevents alert overload before the call. A manager gets dozens of noisy AGMARKNET spreads every morning across 30+ yards; Safe_EV_Sunk_m0 and RT_2F_Min_m0 filter out sporadic Sunday-closed and hyper-volatile traps in milliseconds before the FPO calls destination arhtiyas to confirm live auctions. Moreover, even on pure realized open auctions (exact_d0 trips only, where trader calls confirm openings), Safe_EV_Sunk_m0 cuts ES10 tail risk in half (−₹307.70 vs B2 −₹623.62) while nearly matching open mean (₹924.65 vs ₹930.65, unlike tuned RR which lost −₹94.44).

Q4. Explain exact ES10 and what cluster-key bug your own adversarial review caught (A17 #1).

Unweighted below-quantile averages (the legacy truncated quantile-mean estimator) broke whenever ₹0 abstentions tied at the 10% boundary. Exact ES10 sorts all N episodes worst-first (₹0 on abstentions), sets fractional mass m = 0.10 N = k + f, sums the worst k observations plus fraction f of (k + 1), and divides by m. On clustering: earlier runs forgot State + "|" on bare dest_market, splitting physical markets into 336 duplicate strings instead of 172. Fixing State + "|" and adding policy-specific destination clustering yields exact 173, 172, and 170–173 clusters (23 2-week blocks) and uses strict two-sided Holm-Bonferroni (p_holm_2s = 0.0040)—all primary hypotheses still hold cleanly across all 3 schemes.
