5. Related Work and Surviving Applied Contribution

Five streams frame this retrospective audit:

  1. Spatial Arbitrage and Agri-Information Trials: Parity-bound models (Baulch, 1997) and Indian market integration analyses (Sekhar, 2012; Chatterjee & Kapur, 2017; Negi et al., 2018; Mahajan & Tomar, 2021; Chatterjee, 2023) explain why transport, fees, and market power erode raw spreads. Field evidence spans phone arbitrage gains in fisheries (Jensen, 2007), soybeans (Goyal, 2010), and MIS bargaining (Courtois & Subervie, 2015) to muted SMS effects under credit/middleman ties (Fafchamps & Minten, 2012; Mitra et al., 2018) and e-auction gains (Levi et al., 2020; Levi et al., 2025).
  2. Mandi Price Forecasting and Imputation: Forecasting and advisory papers optimize price RMSE or user workflows (Madaan et al., 2019; Ma et al., 2019; Guo et al., 2020; Ziegler et al., 2019; Ladhar et al., 2023; Biradar & Divyashree, 2026; Manimegalai et al., 2026) or reconstruct blank North-East rows (Deka, 2026), without evaluating sequential missing-arrival turn-backs or cond_ES10.
  3. Decision-Focused Learning, Selective Prediction & Operational Heuristics: Reject-option classifiers and DFL theory model risk-aware decisions (Chow, 1970; El-Yaniv & Wiener, 2010; Geifman & El-Yaniv, 2017; Ban & Rudin, 2019; Elmachtoub & Grigas, 2022; Franc et al., 2023; Mandi et al., 2024) — where the JAIR 80 survey covers predict-then-optimize methodology broadly — while produce export optimization models mean-variance tradeoffs (Vargas et al., 2026). Consistent with 1/N portfolio out-of-sample studies (DeMiguel et al., 2009) and fast-and-frugal heuristics (Gigerenzer & Gaissmaier, 2011), full-season 17-month Mean-Q10 trees achieve the unconstrained ceiling while Safe_EV_Sunk_m0 recovers most of that gain with zero training history.
  4. Partial Identification and Expected Shortfall: Rubin missingness (Rubin, 1976), Manski bounds (Manski, 1990; Horowitz & Manski, 2000), selective-label confounding (Lakkaraju et al., 2017; Kallus & Zhou, 2021), and CVaR/ES10 (Rockafellar & Uryasev, 2000) anchor our coupled/decoupled identification bounds.
  5. Conformal Risk Control Scope: While conformal quantile regression and non-exchangeable calibration provide finite-sample bounds under exchangeability or adaptive weights (Lei et al., 2018; Romano et al., 2019; Gibbs & Candès, 2021; Angelopoulos & Bates, 2023; Barber et al., 2023; Jin & Candès, 2023; Angelopoulos et al., 2024; Lekeufack et al., 2024), our heteroscedastic Split-CQR baseline reduces tail shortfall (cond_ES10 −₹367.09) by surrendering Mean, and non-stationary missing-arrival panels violate exchangeability; we claim zero formal coverage guarantees.

Surviving Contribution: Claiming no methodological novelty, this audit contributes: (1) an open <= t - 1 sequential replay benchmark (171 primary markets + 166-market 2018–2023 archive) isolating hindsight, origin-conditioning, and homoscedastic CQR illusions; (2) a 2×2 orthogonal risk-triage decomposition proving cv28 drives conditional tail truncation while p_dow drives diversion avoidance / Mean lift, plus DS ∩ DB routing separation (+₹65.66 Mean); (3) honest diagnosis and resolution of seasonal ML starvation (17m Mean-Q10 edging Safe_EV_Sunk_m0 without needing safe_ev_feat, while Safe_EV_Sunk_m0 gives a zero-data cold-start rule) and HP graded-apple scale non-invariance (ScaleInv_Safe_EV_24tau); and (4) explicit failure boundaries across coupled Manski cond_ES10, non-collided geocodes (35.23%), outsider Min_Price bargaining, and SUTVA crowding.

6. Explicit Limitations and Non-Claims

  1. Modeled Payoffs ≠ Farm Income: Freight, buyer-paid vs stylized fees, NABCONS cumulative anchors, and 30 qtl scaling are model approximations, not audited truck patti slips. Outsider Min_Price + LCV + 4% arhtiya squeezes Safe_EV Mean to ₹102.62 (34.29% losses).
  2. Blank Row ≠ Closed Gate (Open-MAR & Manski Reversals): Open-MAR reverses Mean (B2 +₹97.01, 25.45% break-even) and worst-case Manski Supp1..3 Means favor B2 (−₹124.95..−₹325.40), even though coupled cond_ES10 stays positive through Supp2 (+₹105.96).
  3. 6 a.m. Call-Priority Alert Filter Only: Never replaces phoning destination traders (Gate J prospective FPO field trial remains open beyond 2026-04-13).
  4. Statistical, Reverse-Survivorship & Scale Limits: Exploratory 2025–2026 inference; HP (B = 12, g_star_block = 1.535 warning, cond_ES10 p = 0.124, F/G_1SD < 0.10 on 79.52%) and Apple (cond_ES10 p = 0.055) remain fragile; 2018–2023 166-market cohort is reverse-survivorship conditioned on 2024 corridor presence with NS 2020/2023 Means; UP shows an unconditional q10 = 0 atom.

Core Scientific Thesis & Methodological Humility

We explicitly distinguish problem/evaluation novelty from methodological novelty: Safe_EV_Sunk_m0, Safe_EV_Phys_m0, and RT_2F_Min_m0 do not claim a new statistical theorem, conformal bound, or stochastic optimization algorithm (mean-variance / tail-risk dispatch and 1/N-style heuristic benchmarks exist in DeMiguel et al. 2009, Gigerenzer & Gaissmaier 2011, and Vargas, Klapp & Mac Cawley 2026). Instead, MandiGuard makes an applied, empirical, and evaluation contribution: diagnosing how day-0 wholesale commodity non-reporting under sequential dispatch creates a severe, state- and weekday-structured censoring trap on public AGMARKNET data, and showing honestly where zero-tuned-parameter domain heuristics sit on the ML/OR Pareto frontier (v7_exp4). Retiring any claim that zero-parameter rules beat all ML, we show earlier ML lags stemmed from 5m starvation (Ridge/HGBR), quadratic INR^2 penalties (OR_Vargas −₹511.42), and homoscedastic CQR (0 switches); fixed heteroscedastic Split-CQR (MG_Intact_matched −₹414.44) and full-season 17m ML (MeanQ10 −₹420.57 / NoSafeEV −₹423.88, CQR80 −₹367.09) achieve stronger cond_ES10, while zero-parameter Safe_EV_Sunk_m0 (0 fitted params, 9 fixed structural choices incl. z = 1.0) captures ~98.8% of 17m MeanQ10 Mean and ~94% of cond_ES10 tail protection with zero training history and pocket-calculator portability.

We likewise do not construct a strawman claiming prior agricultural economists believed wholesale gaps are strictly MAR. Rather, price-transmission and market-information studies focus predominantly on spatial integration on observed price series or portal-level price effects, leaving unmeasured the episode-level arrival payoff distribution when per-shipment physical turn-backs interact with missing day-0 quotes.
