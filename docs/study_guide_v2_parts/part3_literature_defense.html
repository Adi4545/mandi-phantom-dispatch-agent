5. Related Work and Surviving Applied Contribution

Five streams frame this retrospective audit:

  1. Spatial Arbitrage and Agri-Information Trials: Parity-bound models (Baulch, 1997) and Indian market integration analyses (Sekhar, 2012; Chatterjee & Kapur, 2017; Negi et al., 2018; Mahajan & Tomar, 2021; Chatterjee, 2023) explain why transport, fees, and market power erode raw spreads. Field evidence ranges from mobile-phone arbitrage gains in fisheries (Jensen, 2007), soybeans (Goyal, 2010), and MIS bargaining (Courtois & Subervie, 2015) to muted SMS effects under credit/middleman ties (Fafchamps & Minten, 2012; Mitra et al., 2018) and e-auction gains (Levi et al., 2020; Levi et al., 2025).
  2. Mandi Price Forecasting and Imputation: Forecasting and advisory papers optimize price RMSE or user workflows (Madaan et al., 2019; Ma et al., 2019; Guo et al., 2020; Ziegler et al., 2019; Ladhar et al., 2023; Biradar & Divyashree, 2026; Manimegalai et al., 2026) or reconstruct blank North-East rows (Deka, 2026), without evaluating sequential missing-arrival turn-backs or cond_ES10.
  3. Decision-Focused Learning, Selective Prediction & Operational Heuristics: Reject-option and DFL theory optimize risk-aware decisions (Chow, 1970; El-Yaniv & Wiener, 2010; Geifman & El-Yaniv, 2017; Ban & Rudin, 2019; Elmachtoub & Grigas, 2022; Franc et al., 2023; Mandi et al., 2024), while produce export optimization models mean-variance tradeoffs (Vargas et al., 2026). Consistent with 1/N portfolio out-of-sample evidence (DeMiguel et al., 2009) and fast-and-frugal heuristics (Gigerenzer & Gaissmaier, 2011), zero-tuned-parameter triage heuristics outperform both hard activity gates and <= t - 1 ML/OR baselines on cond_ES10.
  4. Partial Identification and Expected Shortfall: Rubin missingness (Rubin, 1976), Manski bounds (Manski, 1990; Horowitz & Manski, 2000), selective-label confounding (Lakkaraju et al., 2017; Kallus & Zhou, 2021), and CVaR/ES10 (Rockafellar & Uryasev, 2000) anchor our coupled/decoupled identification bounds.
  5. Why Conformal Risk Control Is Excluded: Although conformal quantile regression, non-exchangeable calibration, and conformal decision theory offer finite-sample guarantees (Lei et al., 2018; Romano et al., 2019; Gibbs & Candès, 2021; Angelopoulos & Bates, 2023; Barber et al., 2023; Jin & Candès, 2023; Angelopoulos et al., 2024; Lekeufack et al., 2024), Stage I H2 stayed inconclusive and sequential missing-arrival panels violate exchangeability; we claim zero conformal guarantees.

Surviving Contribution: Claiming no methodological novelty, this study delivers a leak-free open benchmark (171-market 2025–2026 + 166-market 2018–2023) showing that two zero-validation-tuned-parameter lower-bound triage heuristics improve both ES10 and dispatched cond_ES10 over equal-age road dispatch and <= t - 1 ML/OR policies under closed-yard, latency (t - 2), Schedule #7 × ERA5/NASA POWER, and stochastic MAR stresses, while mapping exact Manski reversals and subgroup/crowding failure boundaries.

6. Explicit Limitations and Non-Claims

  1. Modeled Payoffs ≠ Farm Income: Freight, buyer-paid vs stylized fees, NABCONS cumulative anchors, and 30 qtl scaling are model approximations, not audited truck patti slips.
  2. Blank Row ≠ Closed Gate (Open-MAR & Manski Reversals): True coupled/decoupled worst-case Manski bounds and Open-MAR reverse Mean in favor of B2, crossing break-even at 25.45% silent-open share (+₹348.61/qtl).
  3. 6 a.m. Alert Filter Ahead of Phone Calls: Never replaces calling destination traders (Gate J prospective FPO trial remains open beyond 2026-04-13 archives).
  4. Statistical & Temporal Limits: Exploratory 2025–2026 calendar exposure; HP (B = 12, p_stud = 0.1864, cond_ES10 p = 0.124) and Apple (cond_ES10 p = 0.055, 41.38% loss) remain fragile; clean 2020/2023 decade Means are NS (p = 0.208/0.077).
  5. General Equilibrium (SUTVA), Offline Corridor Frame, & Non-Conformal Scope: Uncoordinated herding (max = 27) erodes arbitrage unless capped (k <= 2); clean 166-market 2018–2023 offline panel remains inside the 2024 OSRM frame; zero conformal guarantees are claimed.

Core Scientific Thesis & Methodological Humility

We explicitly distinguish problem/evaluation novelty from methodological novelty: Safe_EV_Sunk_m0, Safe_EV_Phys_m0, and RT_2F_Min_m0 do not claim a new statistical theorem, conformal bound, or stochastic optimization algorithm (mean-variance / tail-risk dispatch and 1/N-style heuristic benchmarks exist in DeMiguel et al. 2009, Gigerenzer & Gaissmaier 2011, and Vargas, Klapp & Mac Cawley 2026). Instead, MandiGuard makes an applied, empirical, and evaluation contribution: diagnosing how day-0 wholesale commodity non-reporting under sequential dispatch creates a severe, state- and weekday-structured censoring trap on public AGMARKNET data, and showing that zero-tuned-parameter domain triage heuristics outperform comparable-coverage retrained ML/OR benchmarks on conditional tail risk (cond_ES10) across lookahead-free (171), archival (166 Clean 2018–2023), and adversarial stress regimes (while noting ultra-conservative ML_HGBR_Q20_m0 halves coverage to 32.83%).

We likewise do not construct a strawman claiming prior agricultural economists believed wholesale gaps are strictly MAR. Rather, price-transmission and market-information studies focus predominantly on spatial integration on observed price series or portal-level price effects, leaving unmeasured the episode-level arrival payoff distribution when per-shipment physical turn-backs interact with missing day-0 quotes.
