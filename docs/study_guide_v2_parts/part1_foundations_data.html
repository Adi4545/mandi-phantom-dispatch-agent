Part 1: Problem, Epistemic Table 1, Data Provenance & Settlement Physics

1. Three Research Questions and Honest Methodological Scope

Indian wholesale agricultural markets (mandis) publish daily minimum, modal, and maximum prices by variety on AGMARKNET. While digital price dissemination reduces spoilage when execution is smooth (Jensen, 2007; Goyal, 2010), and targeted market-information services can raise realized farmer prices under symmetric bargaining (Courtois & Subervie, 2015), empirical trials across rural India show that portal quotes frequently fail to deliver arbitrage when transport, fees, volatility, and bargaining frictions bind (Fafchamps & Minten, 2012; Mitra et al., 2018; Chatterjee, 2023). Four operational mechanisms drive our retrospective audit:

  1. Reporting Latency (<= t - 1 or <= t - 2): At 6 a.m. decision day t, same-day auctions have not cleared, and thinner yards publish 1–7 days late (and even slower under multi-day portal lag).
  2. Transfer Frictions and Deterioration: Road freight, single-lane terrain, out-of-district market friction, and ambient transit decay erode nominal spreads (Baulch, 1997; NABCONS, 2022).
  3. Price Volatility and Silent Arrival Boards: Volatile lagged modal prices (dest_price_cv_28d) revert on arrival day t + τ, while blank arrival rows (settle_any_min_dw = NaN) trigger sequential turn-back or fallback losses (Deka, 2026).
  4. Role as an Upstream 6 a.m. Alert Filter: Smallholders routinely phone a destination arhtiya (commission agent) for 30 seconds before dispatch (Jensen, 2007; Ziegler et al., 2019). These heuristics screen out fragile portal alerts before calling traders and never replace live confirmation.

We examine six Indian states (Gujarat, Himachal Pradesh, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Maharashtra, Uttar Pradesh) and four crops (Onion, Potato, Tomato, Apple) through three questions:

  • RQ1 (Screen-Chasing Risk & Settlement Realism): Under a <= t - 1 causal firewall and sequential arrival settlement, how often do lagged portal spreads (> ₹100/qtl) lose money, and how strongly do 3-day hindsight and day-t origin conditioning distort apparent risk?
  • RQ2 (Lower-Bound Triage Heuristics vs Baselines, Hard Gates & ML/OR Models): On equal Top-5 menus (age 1..3), how do two fixed, zero-validation-tuned-parameter lower-bound triage heuristics (Safe_EV_Sunk_m0, Safe_EV_Phys_m0) and calculator rule RT_2F_Min_m0 compare against tuned road dispatch (B2_age_matched), ML-stripped hard gates (MG_T1_T2), reliability-weighted RR_Abstain, and fitted <= t - 1 ML/OR policies (Ridge, HistGradientBoosting, Mean-Variance) on both unconditional ES10 and dispatched conditional cond_ES10?
  • RQ3 (Partial Identification, Structural Stress Tests & Multi-Year Robustness): How sensitive are conclusions to true coupled/decoupled Manski bounds (Manski, 1990; Rubin, 1976), <= t - 2 upload latency, Schedule #7 biological decay, stochastic conditional MAR (Stoch_CondMAR), SUTVA crowding, exact-geocode subsets, small-cluster studentized inference (B = 12 blocks), clean 166-market 2018–2023 pre-period data (N = 383,952) with NASA POWER climate (Sparks, 2018), and Himachal graded apples?

2.2 Tracked Official Data Provenance and Transparency

Six tracked files regenerate all outputs offline cleanly without live network calls:

  1. agmarknet_6state_top_apple_2024_2026.parquet (260,043 raw, 178 core markets): Extracted from the AGMARKNET HuggingFace mirror (1 Jan 2024–13 Apr 2026). Legacy Exp 14 XML parity is 99.42% (171/172) on Tier-1 vs 100.0% on HP Apple. Retaining 369 extreme outlier rows (<₹100 or non-Apple >₹15,000) and pooling 90.3% grade strings (causing a 25.62% variety-grade mismatch on cross-grade pairs) in baseline is audited directly in v7_exp5 (OutlierClean369 & GradeExplicit_6State). Strict 2024-only selection (>= 80 days in 2024, <= 30/state) drops 7 post-2024 lookahead yards and 1 OSRM-distant yard (>500 km), yielding 171 zero-lookahead markets (257,576 raw rows).
  2. agmarknet_6state_decade_2017_2026.parquet (623,363 raw, 2017–2023): To stay offline inside the 2024-active OSRM Top-30 corridor frame while removing all 7 post-2024 lookahead yards from the legacy 173-market extract (N = 389,357), we intersect with 171 to form a clean 166-market non-lookahead pre-period panel (N = 383,952) plus a 115-market 2017-active cohort (N = 325,993), matched against tracked nasa_power_climate_2017_2023.parquet (0.5° grid) (Sparks, 2018).
  3. hp_apple_daily_2025_2026.csv & osrm_corridor_matrix.parquet: 4,427 grade-explicit HP apple rows (42 yards) and 8,147 OSRM road corridors (15.19–499.43 km; 59.50% exact-geocode test rows; 19,362 all-5 exact-geocode episodes; no +4h buffer stored in truck_hours).
  4. state_apmc_and_loss_parameters.csv & mandi_weather_2024_2026.parquet: Because statutory APMC fees are predominantly buyer-paid and PIB 154554 omits UP, our {0.5%, 1.0%, 1.5%} seller tiers represent stylized market-friction tiers (0%–6% stress-tested). Weather variables are excluded completely from all decisions (98.64% ERA5 forecast leak fixed); arrival-day realized ERA5 (0.1°, clipped to [5, 48]°C and [20, 100]% RH) enters settlement only.

2.4 Causal Timing Firewall

Every feature in shortlisting/scoring satisfies dest_quote_date < t and origin_quote_date < t (extending cleanly to <= t - 2 in v7_exp1). Counterfactual tests in pytest (test_timing_and_leakage_r3.py) inject ₹99,999/qtl spikes and drop active yards for all days >= t* and assert bit-for-bit invariance of menus and scores for t <= t*.

3. Payoff Physics, Sequential Settlement, and Decision Rules

3.1 Physical Freight, Stylized Fee Tiers (M9), and Biological Decay

For corridor c = (o → d) in state s and commodity k, freight is Froad(c) = 35.0 + 0.45 · hs · road_km(c) (h_s = 1.45 HP, 1.0 elsewhere). Ambient 20°C / 65% RH reference spoilage over transit truck_hours plus wait days w ∈ {0, 1, 2} (Kader, 2002; Gross et al., 2016) uses:

s(k, w) = min(0.55, bk · max(1, truck_hours + 24 w)24)

with stylized daily rates bk ∈ {0.0325 Tomato, 0.0196 Onion, 0.0164 Apple, 0.0086 Potato} calibrated from NABCONS cumulative market-stage shares (NABCONS, 2022) — contrasted directly in v7_exp2 against biological Schedule #7 (24τdays from harvest/loading + USDA-66 Q10 = 2(Tmax-20)/10). Stylized destination fees τfee ∈ {0, 0.005, 0.010, 0.015} (0 intra-district or unsold return) apply strictly on post-decay destination gross auction sales.

Worked Example 1 (Physical Freight, Decay & Post-Decay Fee): On an out-of-district MP Tomato trip (fee = 1.5%, km = 200, truck_hours = 6, arrival sale ₹2,000 vs local ₹1,500): F = 35 + 0.45(200) = ₹125.00/qtl; s(0) = 0.0325(6/24) = 0.008125; post-decay gross = 2000(0.991875) = ₹1,983.75; net after 1.5% fee = 1983.75(0.985) = ₹1,953.99; net gain = 1953.99 - 125 - 1500 = +₹328.99/qtl.

3.2 Sequential Non-Anticipative Settlement vs Hindsight Lookahead

Starting at w = 0 on arrival t + τ (tau_days in 1..3): if exact variety trades (Modal_Price present), sell at Psale(1 - s(k, w))(1 - τfee) - F - 25w - Ploc, d0; if missing while another variety trades (settle_any_min_dw > 0), wait 1 day (₹25 + decay) for w < 2 (selling at 0.90 * settle_any_min_d2 at w = 2); if zero commodity trade prints on day w, divert home: ΔNetdivert(w) = -div· F - Ploc, d0 s(k, w) - 25w (div = 1.5, zero fee). Blindly waiting 1–2 days regardless of empty day-0 boards is tested in Causal_BlindWait (v7_exp2).

Worked Example 2 (Sequential vs 3-Day Hindsight): If destination Tomato is absent on arrival day 0 yet trades at ₹2,000 on day +1, sequential settlement returns at w = 0 (ΔNet = -1.5(125) - 1500(0.008125) = -₹199.69), whereas 3-day hindsight records +₹239.97 — a ₹439.66/qtl distortion.
