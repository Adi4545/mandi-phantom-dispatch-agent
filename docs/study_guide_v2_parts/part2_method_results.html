3.3 Equal-Menu Baselines, RR-Abstain, and Zero-Parameter Simplification (Safe-EV & RT-2F)

Every rule evaluates the same Top-5 lagged menu (lag_spread > 100) and selects at most one corridor (else abstain with ΔNet = 0). Baselines match dest_lag_age in 1..3 (R4):

  1. B1_lagged_screen: Highest raw lag_spread across age 1..7 (100% dispatch).
  2. B2_age_matched: Road-adjusted score G(c, t) - Froad(c) > ₹200/qtl (where G = Pdest,lag(1 - τfee) - Porig,lag, tuned on validation 70.71% dispatch).
  3. B_simple_activity_matched & MG_matched: Adds 14-day reporting (rep14 >= 0.50) or dual hard gates (rep14 >= 0.50 & dow8 >= 0.35) plus 20°C spoilage, matched to validation 70.71% dispatch (m_mg_match = ₹131.26).
  4. Tuned RR_Abstain (A10, Commit 87a0b62): Mixes open-market payoff Δ̂open and a bounded loss -κ Froad - Porig,lag sref,0 weighted by p̂rep = 0.5 rep28 + 0.5 dow8. Critical Limitation Caught by A17/A18: val optimization hit the grid wall κ* = 5.0 (3.33× physical diversion 1.5), compensating for two missing signals: rep28 over-predicted Sunday mandi closures, and RR omitted auction price volatility. Raising κ further up to 20.0 drives the matched cutoff down to −₹328.99/qtl (showing κ = 5.0 acted as an implicit grid-tuned threshold shift, NOT a pure threshold-free constant).

To eliminate κ tuning, rep28 holiday pollution, and Q10 spoilage lookups simultaneously, Agent A18 designed and locked (PREREG_A18.md, 0 free parameters, fixed S > 0):

  1. Safe_EV_Sunk_m0 (Zero-Parameter Scientific Champion): Combines weekday-specific Laplace reporting frequency pdow = dest_dow_exact_rep_8w ∈ {110, …, 910} with 1-standard-deviation conservative price spread G1SD(c, t) = Pdest,lag(1 - cv28)(1 - τfee) - Porig,lag against sunk one-way freight Froad:
SSafe-EV(c, t) = pdow(c, t) · G1SD(c, t) - Froad(c) > 0   ⟺   G1SDFroad > 1pdow

(And its exact physical counterpart Safe_EV_Phys_m0 using simulator div = 1.5: pdow(G1SD - F) - (1 - pdow) 1.5 F > 0, zero tuning!)

  1. RT_2F_Min_m0 & RT_2F_Dow_m0 (Class-8 Phone-Calculator Rule, NO cv28, NO spoil): Using plain fee-adjusted spread G and pmin = min(rep28, pdow) (or pdow alone), dispatch iff expected revenue clears round-trip freight (2F):
SRT-2F-Min(c, t) = pmin(c, t) · G(c, t) - 2 Froad(c) > 0   ⟺   G2 Froad > 1pmin

Worked Example 3 (Zero-Parameter Safe_EV_Sunk_m0 and Class-8 RT_2F_Min_m0 in 10 Seconds): Take our MP Tomato route (Porig,lag = ₹1,500, Pdest,lag = ₹2,000, τfee = 1.5%, Froad = ₹125, cv28 = 0.15), so G = 2,000(0.985) - 1,500 = +₹470.00/qtl and 1-SD safe spread G1SD = 2,000(0.85)(0.985) - 1,500 = +₹174.50/qtl.

  • Safe_EV_Sunk: 0.90 × 174.50 - 125 = +₹32.05 > 0 ⟹ DISPATCH (Ratio 174.5/125 = 1.40 > 1/0.90 = 1.11).
  • RT_2F_Min (no SD needed): 0.85 × 470.00 - 250 = +₹149.50 > 0 ⟹ DISPATCH.

3.4 Exact Tail Shortfall (ES10) and Corrected Three-Scheme Two-Way Cluster Inference

Following Acerbi–Tasche expected shortfall at α = 0.10 (revision/es10.py), we sort all n episode outcomes x(1) ≤ … ≤ x(n) (0 on abstention) with fractional mass m = 0.10 n = k + f (k = ⌊ m ⌋, f = m - k):

ES10(x) = 1m(∑i=1k x(i) + f · x(k+1))

Worked Example 4 (Exact Fractional ES10): On {1, 2, …, 11}, m = 1.1 (k = 1, f = 0.1), giving ES10 = (1 + 0.1 × 2)/1.1 = 1.2/1.1 ≈ 1.09, unlike unweighted below-quantile averaging (1.50).

After fixing a key-prefixing bug uncovered during adversarial review (bare dest_market previously lacked State| and split physical markets into 336 artificial clusters instead of 172), all CIs and tests use 2,000-replicate paired two-way cluster bootstraps across ALL THREE corrected schemes (23 2-week blocks; Phipson–Smyth +1 correction [phipson2010permutation]):

  1. origin_x_2week: 173 origin State|Market clusters;
  2. b2dest_or_origin_x_2week: 172 State-prefixed B2 destination (or origin) clusters;
  3. policy_dest_or_origin_x_2week: 170–173 policy-specific chosen destination (or origin) clusters.

Crucially, all Holm-Bonferroni FWER adjustments (p_holm_2s) reported throughout this paper are standard TWO-SIDED p-values [holm1979simple] (p_value_two_sided), never unlabeled one-sided p-values.
