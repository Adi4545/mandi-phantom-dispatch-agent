3.3 Equal-Menu Baselines, RR-Abstain, and Zero-Parameter Expected-Value Rules

Every rule evaluates the same Top-5 lagged menu (lag_spread > 100) and selects at most one corridor (else abstains with ΔNet = 0). All comparators share identical quote-age freshness (dest_lag_age in 1..3):

  1. B1_lagged_screen: Highest raw lag_spread across age 1..7 (100% dispatch).
  2. B2_age_matched: Road- and fee-adjusted net spread G(c, t) - Froad(c) > ₹200/qtl, where G = Pdest,lag(1 - τfee) - Porig,lag, tuned on validation (70.71% dispatch).
  3. B_simple_activity_matched & MG_matched: Adds 14-day reporting (rep14 >= 0.50) or dual hard gates (rep14 >= 0.50 & dow8 >= 0.35) plus 20°C spoilage, matched to validation 70.71% dispatch (m_mg_match = ₹131.26).
  4. Tuned RR_Abstain (A10, Commit 87a0b62): Mixes open-market payoff Δ̂open and a bounded loss -κ Froad - Porig,lag sref,0 weighted by p̂rep = 0.5 rep28 + 0.5 dow8. Limitation Identified by A17/A18: Validation optimization hit the grid ceiling κ* = 5.0 (3.33× physical diversion 1.5), compensating for two omissions: rep28 smoothed over Sunday closures, and RR ignored auction price volatility. Extending κ to 20.0 drives the matched cutoff down to −₹328.99/qtl (a10_summary.json), showing that κ = 5.0 acted as a tuned threshold shift rather than a parameter-free constant.

To remove κ tuning, rep28 Sunday smoothing, and biological spoilage lookups simultaneously, we locked three zero-hyperparameter closed-form rules (PREREG_A18.md, fixed S > 0):

  1. Safe_EV_Sunk_m0 (Primary Zero-Parameter Tail-Risk Rule): Combines arrival-weekday Laplace reporting frequency pdow = dest_dow_exact_rep_8w ∈ {110, …, 910} with a 1-standard-deviation conservative spread G1SD(c, t) = Pdest,lag(1 - cv28)(1 - τfee) - Porig,lag against sunk one-way freight Froad:
SSafe-EV(c, t) = pdow(c, t) · G1SD(c, t) - Froad(c) > 0   ⟺   G1SDFroad > 1pdow
  1. Safe_EV_Phys_m0 (Settlement-Matched 1.5F Variant, 0 Tuned Params): Applies the exact baseline diversion multiplier 1.5 directly inside the expected-value inequality with zero tuning:
SSafe-EV-Phys(c, t) = pdow(c, t) · (G1SD(c, t) - Froad(c)) - (1 - pdow(c, t)) · 1.5 Froad(c) > 0
  1. RT_2F_Min_m0 & RT_2F_Dow_m0 (Class-8 Phone-Calculator Rule, NO cv28, NO spoil): Using plain fee-adjusted spread G and pmin = min(rep28, pdow), dispatch iff expected revenue clears round-trip freight (2F):
SRT-2F-Min(c, t) = pmin(c, t) · G(c, t) - 2 Froad(c) > 0   ⟺   G2 Froad > 1pmin

Worked Example 3 (Zero-Parameter Safe_EV_Sunk_m0 and Class-8 RT_2F_Min_m0 in 10 Seconds): Take our MP Tomato route (Porig,lag = ₹1,500, Pdest,lag = ₹2,000, τfee = 1.5%, Froad = ₹125, cv28 = 0.15), giving G = 2,000(0.985) - 1,500 = +₹470.00/qtl and 1-SD safe spread G1SD = 2,000(0.85)(0.985) - 1,500 = +₹174.50/qtl.

  • Steady Yard A (dow8 = 0.90, rep28 = 0.85, so pmin = 0.85):
  • Safe_EV_Sunk: 0.90 × 174.50 - 125 = +₹32.05 > 0 ⟹ DISPATCH (Ratio 174.5/125 = 1.40 > 1/0.90 = 1.11).
  • RT_2F_Min (no volatility needed): 0.85 × 470.00 - 250 = +₹149.50 > 0 ⟹ DISPATCH.
  • Sunday / Thin Yard B (dow8 = 0.20, rep28 = 0.60, pmin = 0.20): Notice B2_age_matched scores Yard B +₹345 (+₹470 - ₹125) identically to Yard A and dispatches. 3.4 Exact Tail Shortfall (ES10) and Corrected Two-Way Cluster Inference (R = 5,000)

Following coherent Conditional Value-at-Risk / expected shortfall at α = 0.10 [rockafellar2000cvar] (revision/es10.py), we sort all n episode outcomes x(1) ≤ … ≤ x(n) (0 on abstention) with fractional tail mass m = 0.10 n = k + f (k = ⌊ m ⌋, f = m - k):

ES10(x) = 1m(∑i=1k x(i) + f · x(k+1))

Worked Example 4 (Exact Fractional ES10): On {1, 2, …, 11}, m = 1.1 (k = 1, f = 0.1), giving ES10 = (1 + 0.1 × 2)/1.1 = 1.2/1.1 ≈ 1.09, unlike unweighted below-quantile averaging (1.50).

After fixing a key-prefixing defect uncovered during review (bare dest_market previously lacked State| and split physical markets into 336 artificial clusters instead of 172), all primary inference uses paired two-way cluster bootstraps with multiplicative market-by-time resampling weights, percentile confidence intervals, Phipson–Smyth +1 two-sided finite-sample p-values [phipson2010permutation], and two-sided Holm–Bonferroni FWER control (p_holm_2s) [holm1979simple] (R = 2,000 in frozen a18_bootstrap.csv and R = 5,000 in v6_bootstrap_5000.csv, minimum two-sided p = 0.0004, 6-test Holm floor 0.0024) across:

  1. origin_x_2week: 173 origin State|Market clusters × 23 fortnights (plus 1-week [45 blocks] and 4-week [12 blocks] sensitivity in Section 4.4);
  2. b2dest_or_origin_x_2week: 172 State-prefixed B2 destination (or origin) clusters × 23 fortnights;
  3. policy_dest_or_origin_x_2week: 171–173 policy-specific chosen destination (or origin) clusters × 23 fortnights.
