Chapter 6 — Stage I Method: MandiGuard Step by Step

Illustration: At 6:00 a.m., suppose a farmer with 30 quintals of onions sees a local price of ₹1,400/qtl while five markets 15–500 km away quote ₹150 to ₹900/qtl more. Stage I MandiGuard (frozen in Git commit e421d15) resolves whether to drive or stay home using an ordered four-step checklist — a verification cascade. Just like airport security checks a boarding pass before scanning luggage, MandiGuard asks cheap safety questions first and immediately prunes failing routes.

Zero LLM, Zero Reinforcement Learning MandiGuard uses no Large Language Model (LLM) and no Reinforcement Learning (RL). Every choice flows deterministically through four gates (T1 → T2 → T3 → T4), one frozen 2024 gradient-boosted tree (HistGradientBoostingRegressor), and an empirical causal 80% conformal cushion. Routes failing the validation-frozen ₹50/qtl net safety margin trigger REFUSE_PHANTOM_SPREAD (SELL_LOCAL, ₹0 risk).

6.2 Every Gate Dissected: Question, Data, Rule, Cost, Ablation & Illustration

Step 0 — Free Morning Board Screen

  • Question & Data: Which 15–500 km destinations beat local price on free public commodity boards (median modal quote forward-filled up to 7 days)?
  • Exact rule & Cost: Keep screen_spread = screen_dest_price − local_price > ₹100/qtl; sort descending; retain top K_max = 5. Cost: 0 calls.
  • Why it exists & Failure impact: Because average freight plus spoilage already totals ₹229.57/qtl (Exp 1), a ₹100/qtl gross filter narrows search to 5 viable candidates without wasting API calls on ~30 markets per state.
  • Illustration: Local Onion pays ₹1,200/qtl. Five yards quote +₹650 to +₹120 (kept); a sixth quotes +₹60 (≤ ₹100, dropped for zero calls).

Gate T1 — Exact-Variety & Arrival-Weekday Vitality

  • Question & Data: Does the destination actively trade my exact variety and stay open on arrival weekday (t + τ) mod 7? Uses causal (≤ t) dest_ref_price, dest_age_days, dest_open_prob_14d, and dest_dow_open_rate.
  • Exact rule & Cost: Pass iff dest_ref_price > 0, dest_age_days ≤ 3, dest_open_prob_14d ≥ 0.50, and dest_dow_open_rate ≥ 0.35 (else VARIETY_OR_STALE or CLOSED_YARD_RISK). Cost: +1 call.
  • Why it exists & Failure impact (Exp 4): Headline boards mix varieties and forward-fill stale quotes for 7 days while yards observe weekly holidays. Deleting T1 is Stage I's worst failure: Holdout mean drops −₹134.05/qtl (₹452.79 → ₹318.74), traps jump 4.2× (16.17% → 67.16%), loss trips hit 42.80%, CVaR_10% falls to −₹987.10/qtl, and calls rise to 11.03! Alone, T1 halts 30.82% of holdout episodes (Exp 7).
  • Illustration: Sanawad quotes +₹1,047/qtl generic Onion, but T1 spots dest_age_days = 99 for Red Onion and prunes in 1 call instead of wasting 3 more calls or sending a truck into a variety trap.

Gate T2 — Highway Freight & Statutory State APMC Fee

  • Question & Data: Does the fresh spread cover real OSRM highway trucking plus GoI state seller fees? Uses road_km, same_district, local_price, and state fee rate γ_{s,c} (0.5% GJ/KA/HP-Veg, 1.0% MH/HP-Apple, 1.5% MP/UP).
  • Exact rule & Cost: Compute F_hat = 35 + 0.45 × m_HP × road_km + I[cross-district] × γ_{s,c} × P_loc (m_HP = 1.45 in HP, 1.0 elsewhere). Prune (FREIGHT_EXCEEDS_SPREAD) if p_fresh − P_loc − F_hat ≤ 0. Cost: +1 call.
  • Why it exists & Failure impact (Exp 4): Straight-line distance misses curved mountain roads (91.59% of HP roads are single-lane). Replacing OSRM with 1.15 × euclid_km trims profit −₹0.44/qtl overall (−₹1.85/qtl in heat/rain) and raises LTR to 10.25%.
  • Illustration: A 320 km cross-district UP route from P_loc = ₹1,400 (1.5% fee = ₹21) costs 35 + 0.45 × 320 + 21 = ₹200/qtl. A +₹180 fresh spread nets −₹20 ≤ 0 → pruned.

Gate T3 — Corridor Heat, Humidity & Heavy-Rain Spoilage

  • Question & Data: Will transit heat, humidity, and rain rot consume remaining profit? Uses day-t NWP forecasts (route_fc_tmax, route_fc_rh, route_fc_rain), NABCONS 20°C rates s_{0,c} (Tomato 3.25%, Onion 1.96%, Apple 1.64%, Potato 0.86%), and dest_intra_spread_14d.
  • Exact rule & Cost: spoil_hat = min(0.55, s_{0,c} × (max(1, truck_hours)/24) × 2^((T_eff−20)/10) × [1 + 0.25 × max(0, (RH_eff−70)/30)] + 0.02 × I[Rain ≥ 35 mm]); if Rain ≥ 35 mm, add quality penalty rain_pen = 0.50 × p_fresh × dest_intra_spread_14d. Prune (WEATHER_SPOILAGE_RISK) if p_fresh × (1 − spoil_hat) − rain_pen − F_hat − P_loc ≤ 0. Cost: +1 call.
  • Why it exists & Failure impact (Exp 4): Open trucks decay 4× faster at 40°C than 20°C, and storms downgrade crates. Without T3, extreme heat/rain LTR rises from 10.10% to 11.39% and CVaR_10% worsens from −₹57.11 to −₹67.49/qtl (though stacking T3 atop T4 trades −₹2.46/qtl overall mean).
  • Illustration: Facing 123 mm forecasted rain on a Karnataka Tomato run, T3 adds +2% rot plus the 50% quality slide toward minimum price, pruning immediately.

Gate T4 — Frozen 2024 ML + 80% Conformal LCB & Final Decision

  • Question & Data: Even on an open yard after freight and rot, what if prices mean-revert during the 1–3 day drive? Uses 17 causal features (FEATURE_COLS), frozen 2024 HGBR point prediction pred_ret, and rolling 400-trip residual quantile q̂_0.80.
  • Exact rule & Cost: Compute lcb_ret = pred_ret − max(0, q̂_0.80), p_arr_lcb = p_fresh × (1 + lcb_ret), and est_net_lcb = p_arr_lcb × (1 − spoil_hat) − rain_pen − F_hat − P_loc.   • Why it exists & Failure impact (Exp 4): Without T4, dispatched loss trips jump from 9.97% to 16.89% and CVaR_10% collapses >10× from −₹37.89 to −₹397.19/qtl.

6.4 How the 2024 Price Model Learns & Freezes

Intuition: What Is a HistGradientBoostingRegressor? Picture 180 experienced mandi brokers in sequence. Broker 1 predicts arrival return from the 17 clues using a shallow tree (max_depth=5). Each next broker fits a small correction tree to prior mistakes, shrunk gently by learning_rate=0.05 (min_samples_leaf=80, l2_regularization=1.0). Binning continuous features into fast histograms lets it score in 0.093 ms/query while splitting commodity_code and state_code natively.

Inside Train_2024 (src/models/arrival_forecaster.py:152-185): (1) Out-of-sample residual seeding: keeping non-null target_ret rows (310,138 of 701,932 rows with arrival_date ≤ 2024-12-31), a temporary model fits Jan–Sep 2024 arrivals (< 2024-10-01) and predicts Oct–Dec 2024 (≥ 2024-10-01) to populate the initial trailing 400-trip error queue without in-sample optimism; (2) Full-2024 freeze: the final point_model fits all settled 2024 rows (target clipped [−0.60, 0.80], seed=20261117) and freezes permanently.

6.5 Causal Conformal LCB & Numeric Walkthrough

Because a 2-day trip leaving June 10 clears only on June 12, peeking at June 12's error on June 10 would leak the future. Our conformal buffer strictly matures residuals via a min-heap only once arrival_date ≤ decision_date:

Causal Conformal LCB & Maturation Buffer r_k = pred_ret(k) − target_ret(k) (push into 400-trip deque B_c(t) ONLY when arrival_date(k) ≤ t) q̂_0.80(c, t) = Quantile_{0.80}(B_c(t)) if |B_c(t)| ≥ 40 else Quantile_{0.80}(B_global(t)) if |B_global(t)| ≥ 20 else 0.12 lcb_ret = pred_ret − max(0.0, q̂_0.80) est_net_lcb = [ p_fresh × (1 + lcb_ret) ] × (1 − spoil_hat) − rain_pen − F_hat − P_loc

Using signed error pred_ret − target_ret targets downside shortfalls specifically. Empirical 80% coverage held at 78.85% on validation and 79.07% on holdout.

  • Why zero holdout overfitting? Mandatory Statistical Disclaimer: Empirical Calibration, NOT Theorem 1 Guarantees Do not claim that Conformal Prediction or Conformal Risk Control (Angelopoulos et al., 2024) gives a mathematical distribution-free Theorem 1 guarantee here. Why? First, daily agricultural market prices across monsoon and harvest seasons are not exchangeable. Second, our denominator n_disp(λ) changes with threshold λ (selective post-decision risk rather than a fixed evaluation denominator). Therefore, (n_l + 1) / (n_d + 1) ≤ 0.095 is used strictly as an empirical validation-calibration heuristic on Val_2025H1 and verified out-of-sample on Holdout, never as a mathematical guarantee.
